## When to use this

Use this on an animal's **Conditions** page:

- add a **condition** the animal has, had or arrived with, such as an ear
  infection, and mark it resolved when it clears up
- add an **allergy**, which everyone handling the animal sees first
- open a **care episode** when the animal is under vet care or hospitalised,
  and close it when that ends

Active allergies and an open care episode show at the top of the animal's
page, for example "Allergies: Chicken, Penicillin" and "Hospitalised at
Downtown Veterinary Clinic since Nov 10, 2026".

## Add a condition or an allergy

1. Open the animal, then the **Medical** tab, then **Conditions**.
2. Under **Problem list**, click **Add condition** or **Add allergy**.
3. Type the **Name** (for an allergy, **Allergic to**).
4. Under **Status**, pick **Active**, **Chronic** or **Resolved**.
5. Fill in what you know: **Onset date**, **Diagnosed by**, **Notes**.
6. Under **Found on**, pick the test or exam that found it, if any.
7. If the condition means a trait the animal's profile should show, such as
   **Deaf**, pick it under **Medical trait it stands for**.
8. Click **Add Condition** (or **Add Allergy**).

You should see "Added to the problem list." The problem list shows allergies
first, then the conditions the animal lives with, then those resolved. What
**Active**, **Chronic** and **Resolved** mean: see the
[glossary](./medical-status-glossary.md#active-chronic-resolved).

A condition found on an exam can also be added from the exam: on **History**,
click **Add a condition found on this exam** under it. **Found on** is filled
in for you.

## Add the trait to the animal

When a condition names a trait, it shows "Stands for the trait: Deaf" and a
button **Add Deaf to the animal**.

1. Click **Add Deaf to the animal**.

You should see "Deaf added to the animal." The condition now reads "on the
animal, citing this condition", and the trait shows on the animal's
**Characteristics**.

If the animal already has the trait from somewhere else, such as an
assessment, the button reads **Cite this condition** instead. Click it to
make the trait point at this condition. You should see "Deaf now cites this
condition."

## Mark a condition resolved

1. Find the condition and click its **…** menu, then **Resolve**.
2. Check the **Resolved date**.
3. Click **Resolve**.

You should see "Condition updated." The condition moves down with the
resolved ones, showing "Resolved" and the day. To fix a detail instead, pick
**Edit** in the same menu. A resolved condition has **Make active** in its
menu to bring it back.

## Open a care episode

1. On **Conditions**, under **Care episodes**, click **Open care episode**.
2. Under **Kind of care**, pick one:
   - **Under vet care**: being treated, here or by an outside vet, without a
     stay.
   - **Hospitalised in house**: kept in the shelter's own medical care.
   - **Hospitalised off site**: staying at a vet clinic.
3. Pick the clinic, if there is one. For **Hospitalised off site**, pick it
   under **Clinic**: it is required. For **Under vet care**, pick it under
   **Clinic (if an outside vet)**, or leave **None**. **Hospitalised in
   house** has no clinic.
4. Check the **Start date**.
5. Type the **Reason**, for example "Ear infection, sedated flush".
6. Click **Open Episode**.

You should see "Care episode opened." The episode shows **Open** with the
day it started, and **Open care episode** is gone until you close it. See
the [glossary](./medical-status-glossary.md#open-care-episode).

To record care that is already over, pick an **End date** too before you
click **Open Episode**. You should see "Care episode recorded." The episode
is saved closed, with its start and end days.

## Close a care episode

1. Under **Care episodes**, on the open episode, click **Close episode**.
2. Pick the **End date**: the day the care ended.
3. Click **Close Episode**.

You should see "Care episode closed." The episode stays on the list with its
start and end days.

## Void a condition, an allergy or a care episode

Void one that should never have been recorded, for example on the wrong
animal. To fix a detail, use **Edit** instead.

1. Click its **…** menu, then **Void…**.
2. Type why, for example "Added to the wrong animal".
3. Click **Void**.

You should see "Condition voided." (or "Care episode voided."). It stays on
the list, struck through, with who voided it and why. A void can't be undone.

## If something goes wrong

- **"The name is required."** Type the name of the condition or allergy.
- **"Chicken is already on the problem list."** It is listed already. Edit
  that one instead.
- **"Give the day it resolved."** A resolved condition needs its **Resolved
  date**.
- **"The resolved date can't be before the onset date."** Check both days.
- **"Say why the animal is under care."** Fill in **Reason**.
- **"Pick the clinic the animal is hospitalised at."** A stay off site needs
  its clinic.
- **"The end date can't be before the start date."** Check both days.
- **"This overlaps another care episode (…)."** An animal's episodes can't
  overlap, though one may start on the day another ended. Check the days.
- **"This animal has left the shelter's care, so no care episode can be opened
  for it."** The animal has an outcome. The same goes for its problem list.
- **No Add condition or Open care episode button.** Keeping the problem list
  and care episodes needs a staff member who manages medical care. Ask them.
- **No Add Deaf to the animal button.** Adding a trait also needs permission
  to manage the animal's characteristics. Ask an admin.
- **The Void button is greyed out.** Type a reason first.
