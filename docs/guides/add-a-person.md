## When to use this

Use this when someone the shelter deals with isn't in the People Directory
yet: a walk-in who wants to adopt, a finder, someone surrendering an animal, a
contact at a partner. The new record has no login.

If the person later signs up on the website with the email you typed, their
login is linked to this record. So only type an address that belongs to them.

To add household details afterwards, or to change a record that exists, see
[Edit a person's details and household](./edit-a-persons-details-and-household.md).

## Steps

1. Look for the person first. In the sidebar, click **People Directory** and
   search for their name, email and phone. See "Find a person and read their
   record" in **Help**.
2. Click **Add Person**. The page **New Person** opens. (If **Add Person** is
   greyed out, your account can't add people.)
3. Type the **Name**.
4. Type an **Email** or a **Phone**. You need at least one of the two, so the
   shelter can reach the person.
5. Add what you know of the address: **Street Address**, **City**, **State**
   and **Zip Code**. They are optional.
6. Click **Create Person**.

You should see "Person created successfully." and the new person's **Profile**
tab opens. If the same person turns out to be in the list already, you'll see
the duplicate warning described below instead.

Some forms, such as the one for adding a foster, offer **Add a new person**
where you pick a person. It asks for the same details.

## Phone numbers

- Type the number however it was given: `(212) 555-0188`, `212-555-0188`,
  `212.555.0188`, `2125550188` or `+1 212 555 0188`. It is saved exactly as
  you typed it and shown that way.
- The app also reads the number in a standard form, so a search or the
  duplicate check finds it whichever way it was written. It reads a number as
  one from the shelter's own country (the United States, unless the app has
  been set up for another country), so type the area code. A number from
  another country needs its country code, for example `+44 20 7946 0958`.
- Spaces, dashes, dots and brackets make no difference.
- The form accepts anything you type, even a number the app can't read, such
  as a short `555-0114` or "call the front desk". It is saved as typed, but
  the duplicate check can't see it and a search finds it only when typed the
  same way. Nothing on the form says so.

## The duplicate warning

When you click **Create Person**, the app checks whether someone else already
has the same email or phone. It never checks the name, because two people can
share one.

- **Same email.** A box titled **Possible duplicate person** says "A person
  with this email already exists" and names them. Emails are compared ignoring
  capital letters. An email can belong to only one person, so the only choice
  is **Use them instead**, which opens their record.
- **Same phone.** The box says "This phone matches an existing contact" and
  shows the name and number. Click **Use them instead** to open that record,
  or **Continue anyway** to create a second person. Use **Continue anyway**
  when two people really share a phone, for example a family.

The same warning appears when you edit one of two records that share a phone
or an email.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Name is required."** Type the **Name**.
- **"Provide an email or phone number so this person can be contacted."**
  Type an **Email** or a **Phone**.
- **"Please enter a valid email address."** Check the **Email**.
- **"A person with this email already exists."** The address is on another
  record that the duplicate warning didn't show, for example one made a moment
  ago or an administrator's. Use a different address, or ask an admin.
- **"Missing or invalid fields. Failed to create person."** Read the red
  messages under the fields and fix them.
- **"Database Error: Failed to create person."** Nothing was saved. Try
  again. If it keeps happening, tell an admin.
- **Access Denied.** Your account can't add people. Ask a staff member.
