## When to use this

An animal's photos are on its **Photos** tab, under **Current Images**. The
first one is marked **Primary photo**: it is the one shown on the animal's
record and first on its listing. Use this guide to add photos, keep the
animal in frame on the website, replace a photo, change which one comes
first, or delete one.

## Add photos

Each photo is cropped to a square before it is saved. The site shows a photo
in several shapes (a circle on the home page, wide frames on the listing and
the animal's page, small squares), and each one cuts off what doesn't fit,
so crop with the animal in the middle. Only the square you choose is saved,
not the whole photo: keep the original on your phone or computer.

1. Open the animal, then the **Photos** tab.
2. Under **Upload New Images**, click **Choose photos** and pick one or more
   photos, or drag the files onto the box. Photos can be JPEG, PNG or WebP.
3. A **Crop photos** box opens on the first photo, with a square drawn over
   it. Move the square over the animal:
   - drag the square to move it;
   - drag its corner to make it bigger or smaller, or use the **Zoom**
     slider (to the right zooms in) or your mouse's scroll wheel;
   - on a phone, drag the square, and use the **Zoom** slider to resize it.
4. Check the previews under **How it will look**: **Home page**, **Listing**,
   **Animal's page** and **Square**. If the animal's head or feet are cut off
   in the **Home page** or **Square** preview, move the square or zoom out.
5. The **Listing** and **Animal's page** previews are wider than the photo,
   so its top or bottom is cut off there. If the animal's head (or feet) is
   cut off, drag either of them down (or up) until the animal is in frame;
   both move together. **Centre** puts the photo back in the middle.
6. Click **Save photo**.

You should see "Photo saved." with the photo's file name at the top of the
screen, and the photo appears last under **Current Images**. Drag it to the
first place to make it the **Primary photo**.

If you added several photos, the next one opens after each save. The list
beside the photo (above it on a phone) shows them all: a tick marks the
ones saved, and clicking one opens it. To leave a photo out, click the **×**
beside it. The box closes after the last photo is saved.

With a keyboard: the square is ready for the keys when a photo opens (its
outline turns dashed; if it isn't, click the square or press Tab until it
is). Use the arrow keys to move it (hold Shift to move further) and **+** or
**-** to resize it. **J** and **K** open the next and previous photo, and
Enter saves. To frame the wide previews, press Tab until the **Listing** or
**Animal's page** preview has an outline, then use the up and down arrow keys
(Home and End go to the top and the bottom).

Closing the box with **×** or Esc while some photos aren't saved asks
"Discard the photos you haven't saved?". Click **Keep cropping** to go back,
or **Discard** to close without them. Photos already saved stay.

## Keep the animal in frame on the website

The website's listing and the animal's page show each photo wider than it
is, so the top or bottom of the photo is cut off there. If the animal's head
is cut off on the website, move the photo up or down in those frames. The
photo itself isn't changed, so this works for every saved photo.

1. Open the animal, then the **Photos** tab.
2. Under **Current Images**, point at the photo. Three buttons appear down
   its right edge: **Adjust framing** (an up-and-down arrow) is the top one.
3. Click **Adjust framing**. An **Adjust framing** box opens with the photo
   as the **Listing** and the **Animal's page** show it.
4. Drag either preview down to show more of the top of the photo, or up to
   show more of the bottom, until the animal is in frame. **Centre** puts it
   back in the middle.
5. Click **Save framing**.

You should see "Framing saved." at the top of the screen. The listing and
the animal's page show the photo that way from now on. Nothing is added to
the activity feed.

To stop without saving, click **Cancel**, **×** or press Esc.

With a keyboard, press Tab until a preview has an outline, then use the up
and down arrow keys (Home and End go to the top and the bottom).

## Replace a photo

Use this when a saved photo is badly cropped (the animal is cut off even in
the square), or when you have a better one. Only the square is ever saved, so
to fix the crop you crop the original photo again, from your phone or
computer. (If the animal is cut off only on the listing or the animal's page,
use **Adjust framing** instead: see above.) The new photo takes the old one's
place: a **Primary photo** stays the primary photo.

1. Open the animal, then the **Photos** tab.
2. Under **Current Images**, point at the photo. A **Replace photo** button
   (a picture with an arrow) appears above the red bin.
3. Click **Replace photo** and pick the new photo. Photos can be JPEG, PNG
   or WebP.
4. A **Replace photo** box opens, with the photo you are replacing beside it
   under **Now**. Move and resize the square over the animal, and frame the
   wide previews, as when adding a photo. The new photo starts centred,
   whatever the old one's framing was.
5. Click **Replace photo**.

You should see "Photo replaced." with the file's name at the top of the
screen. The new photo is in the old one's place under **Current Images**.
The old photo is deleted and can't be restored.

To stop without replacing anything, click **Cancel**, **×** or press Esc.

With a keyboard, the square is ready for the keys when the box opens, as
when adding a photo, and Enter replaces the photo.

The animal's activity feed shows that you replaced a photo.

## Change the order

1. Open the animal, then the **Photos** tab.
2. Under **Current Images**, drag a photo to where it should go. Drag it to
   the first place to make it the **Primary photo**.

The order is saved when you drop the photo; no message pops up. Reload the
page to check it.

With a keyboard, move to the photo with Tab, press Space or Enter to pick it
up, move it with the arrow keys, and press Space or Enter again to drop it.
Press Esc to cancel.

## Delete a photo

1. Point at the photo. A red bin button appears on it.
2. Click the bin button. A box asks "Are you sure you want to delete this
   image? This cannot be undone."
3. Click **Delete Image**.

You should see "Image deleted successfully." The photo leaves the list. If it
was the **Primary photo**, the next one takes its place.

The animal's activity feed shows that you deleted a photo.

A deleted photo can't be restored. To have it back, add the file again (see
**Add photos** above).

## If something goes wrong

- **"This photo list is out of date — it may have changed in another tab.
  Refresh the page and try again."** Someone added or deleted a photo since
  you opened the page. Reload it and drag again.
- **"Database Error: Failed to update the photo order."** or **"Something
  went wrong reordering the photos. Please try again."** The order wasn't
  saved, and the photos go back to their saved order. Drag again; if it
  keeps happening, tell an admin.
- **"Failed to upload image. Please try again."** The photo wasn't added
  (or replaced) and is still open in the box: click **Save photo** (or
  **Replace photo**) again. If it keeps failing, tell an admin.
- **"This file can't be opened as a photo in this browser, so it wasn't
  added. Add a JPEG, PNG or WebP photo. To have an iPhone take JPEG photos,
  set Settings > Camera > Formats to Most Compatible."** The message names
  the file. It isn't a photo, or it is one your browser can't show (an
  iPhone's HEIC photo outside Safari, say), so there is nothing to crop. Any
  other photos you picked with it still open. Save a copy of the photo as a
  JPEG and add that.
- **"Couldn't reach the server, so the image wasn't uploaded. Check your
  connection and try again."** Check that you are online, then click **Save
  photo** (or **Replace photo**) again. The photo is still open in the box.
- **"The cropped photo couldn't be made, so it wasn't uploaded. Try again,
  or close other tabs first."** Your browser ran out of memory making the
  square. Close other tabs and click **Save photo** (or **Replace photo**)
  again.
- **"This photo is too big to upload. Photos can be up to 4 MB. Save a
  smaller copy as a JPEG and add that instead."** A cropped photo is far
  smaller than this, so you should not see it. If you do, tell an admin.
- **"This file isn't a photo. Add a JPEG, PNG or WebP image."** The file
  isn't one of the kinds of photo every browser can show, so it wasn't
  added. Photos cropped in the **Crop photos** box are always one, so you
  should not see this; if you do, tell an admin.
- **"HEIC photos can't be shown in most browsers. Save a copy as a JPEG and
  add that instead. To have an iPhone take JPEG photos, set Settings >
  Camera > Formats to Most Compatible."** The photo is an iPhone's HEIC
  photo, so it wasn't added. Photos cropped in the **Crop photos** box are
  never HEIC, so you should not see this; if you do, tell an admin.
- **"This animal wasn't found, so the photo wasn't added. Reload the page
  and try again."** Reload the page. If the animal is still there, add the
  photo again; if it keeps happening, tell an admin.
- **"This photo wasn't found. It may have been deleted in another tab.
  Reload the page."** Someone deleted the photo since you opened the page,
  so it wasn't deleted, replaced or framed again. Reload it: the photo is
  gone.
- **"This photo has changed since you opened the page. It may have been
  replaced in another tab. Reload the page and try again."** Someone
  replaced the photo since you opened the page, so yours wasn't saved over
  theirs, the photo wasn't deleted, or your framing (made for the old
  photo) wasn't saved. Click **Cancel** if the **Replace photo** or **Adjust
  framing** box is open, reload the page, look at the photo now there, and
  replace, frame or delete it again if it still needs it.
- **"The framing wasn't saved. Try again."** The **Adjust framing** box
  stays open: click **Save framing** again. If it keeps happening, tell an
  admin.
- **"Couldn't reach the server, so the framing wasn't saved. Check your
  connection and try again."** Check that you are online, then click **Save
  framing** again. If it keeps happening, reload the page: your sign-in may
  have run out.
- **"The photo's framing wasn't understood. Reload the page and try
  again."** Reload the page and frame the photo again. If it keeps
  happening, tell an admin.
- **"This file can't be opened as a photo in this browser, so the photo
  wasn't replaced. Choose a JPEG, PNG or WebP photo. To have an iPhone take
  JPEG photos, set Settings > Camera > Formats to Most Compatible."** The
  message names the file. As when adding a photo: save a copy as a JPEG and
  replace the photo with that.
- **"Failed to delete image."** The photo wasn't deleted and is still in the
  list. Try again; if it keeps happening, tell an admin.
- **"Unauthorized: You must be logged in."** Your sign-in has run out.
  Reload the page, sign in, and add the photo again.
- **"Your account is not associated with a person record."** Your account
  isn't set up to record changes. Tell an admin.
