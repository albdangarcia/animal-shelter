## When to use this

An animal's photos are on its **Photos** tab, under **Current Images**. The
first one is marked **Primary photo**: it is the one shown on the animal's
record and first on its listing. Use this guide to change which one comes
first, or to delete one.

To add photos, see
[Add and crop an animal's photos](./add-and-crop-an-animals-photos.md). If
the animal is cut off in a photo, fix it instead of deleting it: see
[Replace or reframe an animal's photo](./replace-or-reframe-an-animals-photo.md).

## Change the order

1. Open the animal, then the **Photos** tab.
2. Under **Current Images**, drag a photo to where it should go. Drag it to
   the first place to make it the **Primary photo**.

The order is saved when you drop the photo; no message pops up. Reload the
page to check it.

With a keyboard, move to the photo with Tab, press Space or Enter to pick it
up, move it with the arrow keys, and press Space or Enter again to drop it.
Press Esc to cancel.

## Delete a photo

1. Open the animal, then the **Photos** tab.
2. Under **Current Images**, point at the photo. A red bin button appears on
   it.
3. Click the bin button. A box asks "Are you sure you want to delete this
   image? This cannot be undone."
4. Click **Delete Image**.

You should see "Image deleted successfully." The photo leaves the list. If it
was the **Primary photo**, the next one takes its place. The animal's
activity feed shows that you deleted a photo.

A deleted photo can't be restored. To have it back, add the file again.

## If something goes wrong

- **"This photo list is out of date — it may have changed in another tab.
  Refresh the page and try again."** Someone added or deleted a photo since
  you opened the page. Reload it and drag again.
- **"Database Error: Failed to update the photo order."** or **"Something
  went wrong reordering the photos. Please try again."** The order wasn't
  saved, and the photos go back to their saved order. Drag again; if it
  keeps happening, tell an admin.
- **"This photo wasn't found. It may have been deleted in another tab.
  Reload the page."** Someone deleted the photo since you opened the page.
  Reload it: the photo is gone.
- **"This photo has changed since you opened the page. It may have been
  replaced in another tab. Reload the page and try again."** Someone
  replaced the photo since you opened the page, so it wasn't deleted. Reload
  the page, look at the photo now there, and delete it again if you still
  want to.
- **"Failed to delete image."** The photo wasn't deleted and is still in the
  list. Try again; if it keeps happening, tell an admin.
- **"Your account is not associated with a person record."** Your account
  isn't set up to record changes. Tell an admin.
