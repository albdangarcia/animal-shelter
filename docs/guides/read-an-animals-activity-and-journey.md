## When to use this

Two tabs on an animal's record say what has happened to it:

- **Activity** lists every change to the record, newest first: who made it,
  when, and what changed. Use it to find out who changed something.
- **Journey** is the animal's story at the shelter, oldest first: each time
  it came in or left, changes to its listing, and its foster stays.

## Read the activity

1. Open the animal. The **Activity** tab opens first.
2. Each line says who did what and when, for example "Olivia Chen moved this
   animal • 25 seconds ago".
3. Click **Show details** under a line to see what changed, for example
   "Moved to Dog block A · A-2." or "Task "Weigh-in" reassigned from Sam
   Rivera to Olivia Chen." Click **Hide details** to close it.
4. Older changes are on the next pages. Use the page numbers, **Previous**
   and **Next** at the bottom.

Above the list, staff who can see the animal's assessments, characteristics
and medical record also see **Readiness**: what is still outstanding before
the animal is ready for adoption.

## Read the journey

1. Open the animal, then the **Journey** tab.

Each event has a title, a sentence, who recorded it and the day:

- **Intake** with its type, for example "Intake: Owner Surrender" and
  "Charlie was admitted to the shelter."
- **Outcome**, for example "Outcome: Adopted" and "Charlie has been adopted
  and left the shelter." An outcome that was later reversed has "(reversed)"
  after its title, and the reversal is listed as **Outcome: Reversed**.
- **Status Change**, for example "Listing status changed from DRAFT to
  PUBLISHED."
- **Foster: Placed** and **Foster: Returned**.

Moves between units, notes, tasks and medical care aren't on the journey.
They are on the **Activity** tab.

## If something goes wrong

- **Access Denied on the Activity or Journey tab.** Your account can't see
  that tab. Ask an admin.
- **A detail in an intake is wrong.** Staff who take animals in can correct
  the intake from the **Intakes** list. The correction then shows on the
  **Activity** tab.
