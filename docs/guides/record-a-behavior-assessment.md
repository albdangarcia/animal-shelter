## When to use this

An assessment records a structured check on a form the shelter set up:
**Intake Behavioral**, **Cat Test**, **Dog-to-Dog Introduction**, **Handling
Sensitivity** or **Daily Rounds**. Some forms are for dogs only, so a cat is
offered fewer. The **Readiness** box on the animal's **Activity** tab lists
the ones it still needs.

Some answers suggest a characteristic. For example, "Dog-social" under
**Recommendation** on a dog introduction suggests "Good with other dogs".
Nothing is added to the animal until someone acts on the suggestion.

## Record an assessment

1. Open the animal, then the **Assessments** tab.
2. Click **Record Assessment**. The page **Record an assessment** opens.
3. Under **Template**, pick the kind of check. The questions change to
   match. To read what it covers, point at or click the ⓘ beside
   **Template**.
4. Check **Observed on**. It starts on today.
5. Answer each question. The ones marked * are required. To add detail to an
   answer, type it in the **Note** box beside it (under it in a narrow
   window).
6. Under **Signal**, pick how the animal did: **No concerns**, **Monitor**,
   **Follow up** or **Escalate**. It starts on **No concerns**. If an answer
   is a concern, a red **Concerning finding** box names it and says "This
   will be recorded as at least "Monitor"": the assessment is saved with that
   signal even if you leave **No concerns** picked.
7. Write a **Summary**, for example "Played well with Daisy in the yard."
8. Click **Record assessment**.

You should see "Assessment recorded." The **Assessments** tab opens, with the
new assessment at the top: the form's name, the day, who recorded it, the
signal and the summary. To narrow the list, use **Signal** or **Status**, or
change **Newest First** to **Oldest First**.

## Add the characteristics it suggests

1. On the **Assessments** tab, click the assessment to open it.
2. Under **Characteristics these findings suggest**, each trait is listed
   with the answer behind it, for example "Good with other dogs" and "From
   “Recommendation”: Dog-social".
3. Click **Add to animal**.

You should see "Good with other dogs added to the animal." (with the trait).
On the **Characteristics** tab, the trait reads "Added by Olivia Chen on
November 16, 2026 — from the Dog-to-Dog Introduction assessment of November
16, 2026".

If the animal already has the trait, the button reads **Cite this
assessment** instead. Click it to make this assessment the trait's source.
You should see "Good with other dogs re-cited to this assessment."

If another assessment found something against the trait, a warning shows
under the suggestion. It doesn't stop you adding it.

## Who sees what

Recording an assessment and adding a characteristic are two separate
permissions.

- **You can record assessments but not manage characteristics.** You see the
  suggestions, with no **Add to animal** or **Cite this assessment** button.
  Ask a staff member who manages characteristics to add them.
- **You can manage characteristics but not record assessments.** **Record
  Assessment** is greyed out, but you can open any assessment and add or
  cite what it suggests.

## Edit an assessment

1. Open the assessment and click **Edit**. On the **Assessments** tab, the
   assessment's **…** button, then **Edit**, does the same.
2. Change the answers, the **Signal** or the **Summary**. The template can't
   be changed: record a new assessment instead.
3. Click **Save changes**.

You should see "Assessment updated."

## Delete and restore an assessment

1. Open the assessment and click **Delete**, or on the **Assessments** tab
   click its **…** button, then **Delete**.

You should see "Assessment deleted." Click **Undo** on that message to bring
it straight back. If a trait cites it, the message says so, for example
"Assessment deleted. Good with other dogs stays on the animal, citing the
deleted assessment." The trait then reads "(deleted)" after the
assessment's name.

A deleted assessment isn't gone for good. To see it, click **Status** and
tick **Deleted**: it is listed, marked **Deleted**. Anyone who can record
assessments can restore it:

1. Click the deleted assessment's **…** button, then **Restore**. You can
   also open it and click **Restore**.

You should see "Assessment restored."

## If something goes wrong

- **A question says, for example, "Greeting style is required."** Answer it.
  The assessment isn't recorded until every required question has an answer.
- **"The Cat Test is a Dog assessment, but Misty is a Cat."** (with the form,
  the animal and their species) The animal's species was changed while you had
  the form open. Go back to the animal and click **Record Assessment** again:
  the list then only offers forms for its species.
- **"This assessment was recorded against a template version that can no
  longer be edited."** The form has changed since. Record a new assessment
  instead.
- **"A deleted assessment can't source characteristics."** Restore the
  assessment first.
- **"This assessment no longer proposes that characteristic."** The answer
  changed since you opened the page. Reload it.
- **Record Assessment is greyed out, and there is no … button on the
  assessments.** Your account can't record assessments. Ask a staff member.
- **"Database Error: Failed to record the assessment."** Nothing was saved.
  Try again; if it keeps happening, tell an admin.
