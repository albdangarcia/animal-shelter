## When to use this

Use this when the shelter starts working with an outside organisation, or
when one changes its address, phone or name. A partner has to be in the
directory before you can pick it as the source of an animal or its
destination.

The people who work at a partner are its contacts. Add them afterwards: see
[Manage a partner's contacts](./manage-a-partners-contacts.md). Dated
comments go in notes: see
[Add, edit or delete a note on a partner](./add-edit-or-delete-a-note-on-a-partner.md).

## What the type is for

Pick the **Type** that fits: **Shelter**, **Rescue Group**, **Vet Clinic** or
**Government Agency**. It shows as a badge and you can filter by it. It
changes one thing: only partners of type **Vet Clinic** that are **Active** are
offered as the **Vet clinic** wherever the shelter records care given outside,
such as a vaccine, a hospital stay off site or a vet visit report. If a clinic
is missing there, check its type and that it is active.

Where else a partner shows up:

- **Source Partner**, on an animal taken in by **Transfer In**. See "Take in a
  new animal" in **Help**.
- **Destination Partner**, on an outcome of **Transfer Out**. See "Record an
  adoption, transfer out, return to owner or other outcome" in **Help**.
- Those two lists show every partner of any type, even a retired one. The
  partner's **Animal History** tab then lists the animals.
- The intake report lists the **Top transfer-in partners**, and the search box
  at the top of every page finds partners by name, email or phone.

## Add a partner

1. In the sidebar, click **Partner Directory**, then **Add Partner**. The page
   **New Partner** opens. (If **Add Partner** is greyed out, your account
   can't add partners.)
2. Search the list first. A partner's name must be different from every other,
   and capital letters don't count, so "Second Chance Rescue" and "second
   chance rescue" are the same name. A retired partner keeps its name, so
   check for it too. Don't add one twice.
3. Type the **Name** and pick the **Type**.
4. Fill in what you know: **Email**, **Phone**, **Website** (it must start
   with `https://`), **Street Address**, **City**, **State** and
   **Zip Code**. They are optional. The phone is saved as typed.
5. Leave **Country** on **United States** for a partner in the US. For a
   partner elsewhere, see [A partner outside the US](#a-partner-outside-the-us).
6. Leave **Active partner** ticked.
7. Under **Quick note**, write a short line to keep pinned on the partner,
   such as "Transfers weekdays only." This is optional.
8. Click **Create Partner**.

You should see "Partner created successfully." and the new partner's page
opens, showing **Active**.

## A partner outside the US

A partner in Puerto Rico, Guam or another US territory is in the US. Leave
**Country** on **United States** and pick the territory under **State**.

Pick the partner's country under **Country**. You can type the first letters
of its name to jump to it. **State** then disappears, and a state already
picked is cleared: only a partner in the United States has a state. Put a
postal code in **Zip Code**.

The partner's page shows the country at the end of its address, and the
**State / Country** column of the list shows the country, for example
"Canada". A US partner shows its state there.

The country and state tell whether a transfer is in state, out of state or
outside the US, against the shelter's own state. An admin sets that: see
"Set the shelter's state" in **Help**.

## Edit a partner

1. Open the partner and click **Edit Partner**. You can also click **…** at
   the end of its row in the list, then **Edit Partner Info**. The page **Edit
   Partner** opens.
2. Change what you need. To retire a partner without deleting it, untick
   **Active partner**. To bring it back, tick it again.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

You should see "Partner updated successfully." and you go back to the
partner's page, or to the list if you started from there. A retired partner
shows **Inactive** and is no longer offered as a vet clinic. Its history stays.
There is no way to delete a partner.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Name is required."** Type the **Name**.
- **"Please select a partner type."** Pick a **Type**.
- **"Please enter a valid email address."** Check the **Email**.
- **"Please enter a valid URL (including https://)."** Type the **Website**
  with `https://` in front, for example `https://example.org`.
- **"A partner with this name already exists."** Another partner, retired or
  not, has this name. Capital letters don't make it different, so "walk vet
  clinic" is the same as "Walk Vet Clinic". Search for it in the list and use
  that partner, or give this one a different name. The same message appears
  when you rename a partner to another one's name.
- **"Partner not found."** The partner was removed. Go back to **Partner
  Directory**.
- **"Database Error: Failed to create partner."** or **"Database Error: Failed
  to update partner."** Nothing was saved. Try again. If it keeps happening,
  tell an admin.
- **Access Denied.** Your account can't change partners. Ask a staff member.
