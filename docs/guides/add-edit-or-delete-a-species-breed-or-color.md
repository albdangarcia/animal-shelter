## When to use this

Species, breeds and colors are the lists staff pick from on the Add Animal
form and when they edit an animal: **Species**, **Breed**, **Primary Color**
and **Additional Colors**. Each breed belongs to one species. Use this guide
to add to those lists, change an entry, or take one out of use.

To change one animal's breed or color, edit the animal instead.

## Add a species, breed or color

1. In the sidebar, open **Settings**, then the **Animal Taxonomy** card. It has
   a **Colors**, a **Species** and a **Breeds** section.
2. Click **Add Color**, **Add Species** or **Add Breed**.
3. Type the **Name**, for example "Merle" or "Ferret".
4. For a breed, also pick its **Species**. Only species that aren't deleted
   are listed. **Typical Adult Size** is optional: pick **Small**, **Medium**,
   **Large** or **Extra Large**, or leave **Not specified**.
5. Click **Create Color**, **Create Species** or **Create Breed**.

You should see "Color created successfully.", "Species created successfully."
or "Breed created successfully." The entry appears in its section. A breed is
listed under its species with its size, for example "Small", or "No typical
size".

Staff can pick it on the Add Animal form straight away. A breed is offered
once its species is picked, and picking it fills in **Expected Adult Size**
with the breed's typical size.

## Edit a species, breed or color

1. Click the **…** button beside the entry, then **Edit**.
2. Change the **Name**. For a breed, you can also change its **Species** or
   **Typical Adult Size**.
3. Click **Update Color**, **Update Species** or **Update Breed**.

You should see "Color updated successfully.", "Species updated
successfully." or "Breed updated successfully.", and the section shows the
change.

If you move a breed to another species, animals of the old species keep it
on their page. When you next edit one of them, **Breed** is empty. Pick one
of its species' breeds to save.

## Delete a species, breed or color

Delete an entry staff should stop picking. Nothing is lost: you can restore
it later.

1. Click the **…** button beside the entry, then **Delete**. There is no
   confirmation, so check the name first.

You should see "Color deleted successfully.", "Species deleted
successfully." or "Breed deleted successfully.", with an **Undo** button. The
entry stays in its section, faded, marked **Deleted**. The Add Animal form no
longer offers it, and nor does editing an animal.

A species can be deleted only when none of its breeds is live and no animal
is of that species. Delete its breeds first.

Animals and fosters that already have a deleted entry:

- **A deleted breed** no longer shows on the animal's page. When you next
  edit the animal, **Breed** is empty. Pick another breed to save.
- **A deleted primary color** still shows on the animal's page. When you next
  edit the animal, **Primary Color** is empty. Pick another color to save.
- **A deleted additional color** no longer shows on the animal's page. The
  next time someone saves the animal, it is removed from the animal for good:
  restoring the color afterwards doesn't bring it back there.
- **A deleted species** stays on a foster who lists it. To take it off, open
  the foster's **Fostering** tab, click **Edit Capabilities**, untick it under
  **Species you can foster**, and click **Save**.

## Bring one back

1. Click **Undo** on the message, or click the **…** button beside the entry,
   then **Restore**.

You should see "Color restored successfully.", "Species restored
successfully." or "Breed restored successfully." The entry is no longer
faded, and the Add Animal form offers it again. To bring back a breed of a
deleted species, restore the species first.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Name is required."** Type a name.
- **"A color with that name already exists."** or **"A species with that name
  already exists."** No two live colors, or live species, can share a name,
  in any capitals. Pick another name, or edit the one that has it. If one
  marked **Deleted** has the name, restore it instead.
- **"A breed with that name already exists for this species."** No two live
  breeds of a species can share a name, in any capitals. Another species can
  have a breed with the same name.
- **"This species can't be deleted because it still has breeds or animals
  associated with it."** Delete each of its breeds first. A species that
  animals have can't be deleted.
