## When to use this

Use a note for anything staff should know about the animal that has no field
of its own, for example "Pulls hard on the leash near the front gate." Each
note has a category: **Behavioral**, **Medical**, **Feeding**, **General**,
**Adoption Update** or **Foster Update**.

Medical care itself goes on the **Medical** tab, and a job someone has to do
is a task.

## Add a note

1. Open the animal, then the **Notes** tab. Notes are listed newest first.
2. Click **Add Note**. The box **Add Note** opens.
3. Pick the **Category**.
4. Type the note under **Content**.
5. Click **Create Note**.

You should see "Note created successfully." The note is at the top of the
list, with its category, your name and when you wrote it, for example
"Olivia Chen · 1 second ago".

To see one kind of note, click **Category** and tick it. To see the oldest
first, change **Newest First** to **Oldest First**.

## Edit a note

1. Click the **…** button on the note, then **Edit**. The box **Edit Note**
   opens.
2. Change the **Category** or the **Content**.
3. Click **Update Note**.

You should see "Note updated successfully." Under the note, a line says who
last changed it and when, for example "edited by Olivia Chen · 0 seconds
ago". Deleting or restoring a note updates that line too.

## Delete and restore a note

1. Click the **…** button on the note, then **Delete**.

You should see "Note deleted successfully." Click **Undo** on that message to
bring it straight back. The note leaves the list.

A deleted note isn't gone for good. To see it, click **Status** and tick
**Deleted**: it is listed, marked **Deleted**. Anyone who can write notes can
restore it:

1. Click the **…** button on the deleted note, then **Restore**.

You should see "Note restored successfully."

## If something goes wrong

- **"Content cannot be empty."** Type the note under **Content**.
- **"No changes to save."** You clicked **Update Note** without changing
  anything. Nothing was saved.
- **"Database Error: Failed to create notes."** Nothing was saved. Try
  again; if it keeps happening, tell an admin.
