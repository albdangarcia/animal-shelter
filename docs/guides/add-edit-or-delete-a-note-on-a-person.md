## When to use this

Use a note for anything staff should know about a person that has no field of
its own, for example "Prefers calls after 5pm. Allergic to cats." Notes about
an animal go on the animal: see "Add, edit or delete a note" in **Help**. Contact details
and the household are fields: see
[Edit a person's details and household](./edit-a-persons-details-and-household.md).

Everyone who can open the People Directory can read these notes, volunteers
included. Don't write anything you wouldn't want them to read.

## Add a note

1. Open the person: in the sidebar, click **People Directory**, then click
   their name. Click the **Notes** tab. Notes are listed newest first.
2. Click **Add Note**. The box **Add Note** opens.
3. Type the note under **Content**.
4. Click **Create Note**.

You should see "Note created successfully." The note is at the top of the
list, with your name and when you wrote it, for example "Olivia Chen · 0
seconds ago". Unlike an animal's notes, these have no category.

To see the oldest first, change **Newest First** to **Oldest First**.

The app writes some notes itself, for example when a login is linked to the
person. They show **Unknown User** or the admin's name as the author.

## Edit a note

1. Point at the note and click the **…** button at its top right, then
   **Edit**. The box **Edit Note** opens.
2. Change the **Content**.
3. Click **Update Note**.

You should see "Note updated successfully." Under the note, a line says who
last changed it and when, for example "edited by Olivia Chen · 0 seconds ago".
Deleting or restoring a note updates that line too.

## Delete and restore a note

1. Click the **…** button on the note, then **Delete**.

You should see "Note deleted." Click **Undo** on that message to bring it
straight back. The note leaves the list.

A deleted note isn't gone for good. To see it, click **Status** and tick
**Deleted**: it is listed, marked **Deleted**. Anyone who can write notes can
restore it:

1. Click the **…** button on the deleted note, then **Restore**.

You should see "Note restored." Untick **Deleted** under **Status** to go back
to the notes that are in use.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Content cannot be empty."** Type the note under **Content**.
- **"No changes to save."** You clicked **Update Note** without changing
  anything. Nothing was saved.
- **"Database Error: Failed to create note."** or **"Database Error: Failed
  to update note."** Nothing was saved. Try again. If it keeps happening, tell
  an admin.
- **"Failed to delete note."** or **"Failed to restore note."** Nothing
  changed. Try again. The message looks like the green success message, with
  an **Undo** button on a delete, so read the words. If the note is still in
  the list, the delete failed.
- **Add Note** is greyed out, and the notes have no **…** button. Your account
  can read notes but not write them. Ask a staff member.
