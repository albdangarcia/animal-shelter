## When to use this

Use this if you would like to foster: to look after a shelter animal at home
for a while. You fill in one form about you, your home and what you can
take on. The shelter reviews it. Sending it doesn't mean an animal is on its
way: the shelter looks at it first and gets in touch.

This is not an adoption application. To adopt, use **My Adoption
Applications**.

You can have one foster application at a time.

## Apply

1. In the sidebar, click **My Foster Application**.
2. The form **My foster application** opens in three sections. Fields marked
   with a star are required.
   - **Applicant Information**: your name, email, phone, address, city, state
     and ZIP code. The email is the one the shelter will use. It doesn't
     change the email you sign in with.
   - **Household & Lifestyle**: **Living Situation**, **Household Size**
     (including you), whether you have a yard, whether there are children,
     any other animals at home, and your **Experience with Animals**.
   - **Foster Capabilities**: under **Species you can foster**, pick one or
     more. Then **Max animals at once**, and yes or no for **Quarantine space
     available?**, **Can give oral medications?**, **Can bottle-feed?**,
     **Can transport animals?**, **Willing to foster medical cases?** and
     **Willing to foster hospice cases?**. **Not specified** is fine. Add
     anything about your time under **Availability Notes**, such as travel.
3. Read your answers through. The form may start with details the shelter
   already has for you, so check that they are still true.
4. Click **Submit Application**.

You should see "Foster application submitted successfully." The page now
reads **My Foster Application** with **Pending**, the day you sent it, and your
answers. The shelter saves your name, phone and address to your profile too.

## Follow your application

Open **My Foster Application** any time. The status is next to the title, and
**Status History** at the bottom lists each change with the shelter's reason.

- **Pending**: sent and waiting.
- **Reviewing**: the shelter has picked it up.
- **Waitlisted**: the shelter is keeping your application for later.
- **Approved**: you are on the shelter's list of fosters. The page now reads
  "You're an approved foster", with a link to **My Foster Animals**. You will
  hear from the shelter when an animal needs a home. Being approved doesn't mean
  an animal has been placed with you.
- **Rejected**: the shelter said no. The reason is under **Status History**. You can
  send a new application: the form appears under it.
- **Withdrawn**: you withdrew it. The form appears under it too.

## Withdraw your application

1. On **My Foster Application**, click **Withdraw Application**.
2. A box asks "Withdraw your foster application?" Click **Withdraw**.

You should see "Foster application withdrawn successfully." The status reads
**Withdrawn**, and you can send a new application whenever you like.

Once you are an approved foster and active, the page shows "You're an approved
foster" and no **Withdraw Application** button. Withdrawing never takes you off
the shelter's list. To stop fostering, contact the shelter.

## If something goes wrong

- **A message under a field**, for example "Select at least one species you can
  foster.", "Living situation is required.", "Animal experience is required",
  "Invalid email address.", "Invalid ZIP code." (five digits) or "Landlord
  permission is required when renting." Fix the field and click **Submit
  Application** again.
- **"You already have a foster application in progress. Please wait for it to be
  reviewed, or withdraw it before applying again."** Open **My Foster
  Application** to see it.
- **"Missing or invalid fields. Failed to submit foster application."** Look for
  a field with a message under it.
- **"Database Error: Failed to submit application. Please try again."** Nothing
  was sent. Try again. If it keeps happening, contact the shelter.
- **"Cannot withdraw application. Its status is currently "Rejected"."** (or
  "Withdrawn") There is nothing to withdraw.
- **"Foster application not found."** or **"Database Error: Failed to withdraw
  foster application."** Nothing changed. Reload and try again, or contact the
  shelter.
