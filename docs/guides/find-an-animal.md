## When to use this

The **Animals** list has every animal the shelter has recorded, including
those that have left. Use it to find an animal and open its record. An animal
that came back is on it once; to see each time it came in, use the
**Intakes** list instead.

## Steps

1. In the sidebar, open **Animals**. The animals added most recently are at
   the top.
2. Type part of the name in **Filter by animal name...**. The list shows only
   animals whose name contains it. Several animals can share a name, so check
   the status, **Age** and **Sex** on each row.
3. To see one listing status, click **Status** and tick **Draft**,
   **Published**, **Pending Adoption** or **Archived**. **Archived** animals
   have left the shelter. Tick more than one to see them together.
4. To see one sex, click **Sex** and tick **Male**, **Female** or
   **Unknown**.
5. Once a name or a filter is set, a **Reset** button appears. Click it to
   see the whole list again.
6. Click the animal's name to open its record. You can also click the **…**
   button at the end of the row, then **Profile**.

Each row shows the animal's listing status, then its **Name**, **Age**,
**Sex** and **Size** (its expected adult size). To sort by a column, click
its name, then **Asc** or **Desc**. Use **Customize Columns** (**Columns**
in a narrow window) to hide columns, and **Rows per page** to show more at once.

## What an animal's record shows

The top of the record shows the animal's photo, species, age, sex and size,
then where it is housed (**Location**), its latest weight and its medical
status. Under it are the tabs:

- **Activity**: every change made to the record, newest first.
- **Tasks**: the to-dos for this animal.
- **Journey**: when it came in, left, or went to a foster.
- **Notes**: staff notes, such as behavior or feeding.
- **Medical**: vaccines, treatments, medications, conditions and vitals.
- **Characteristics**: traits such as "Good with kids".
- **Assessments**: behavior checks such as a cat test.
- **Applications**: adoption applications for this animal.
- **Photos**: its pictures.

## If something goes wrong

- **The animal isn't on the list.** The filter matches the name only. Check
  the spelling, and check **Status** and **Sex** in case a filter is set.
  Click **Reset** to clear them.
