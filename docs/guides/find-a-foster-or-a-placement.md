## When to use this

Use this to look something up: who is fostering, who has room for another
animal, which foster has an animal, or where a foster application stands. It
only reads. To change something, you need an account that manages fosters (see the
guides under **Related**).

## Find a foster on the roster

1. In the sidebar, click **Fosters**. The **Foster Roster** lists approved
   fosters with **Name**, **Status**, **Species**, **Capacity** and **Fostering
   Now**.
2. Type under **Filter by name, email, or phone...**, or narrow the list with
   **Status**, **Species** or **Capacity**.

**Capacity** reads, for example, **1/2**: one animal with them, out of
at most two. Under **Capacity**, **Has Capacity** shows only **Active** fosters
with room. **Active**, **Paused** and **Inactive** are explained in
"Pause, deactivate or change a foster" in **Help**.

## See a foster's placements

1. On the roster, click the foster's name. Or click the **…** button on their
   row, then **View Profile**.
2. The **Fostering** tab opens. **Foster Status** shows what they can take.
   **Current Placements** lists the animals with them now and the placement
   type. **Placement History** lists every placement: **Animal**, **Type**,
   **Start**, **End** (or **Ongoing**) and **Return Reason**, newest first.

A person with no foster profile has "They have no foster profile yet." on
this tab.

A **Return Reason** reads **Returned To Shelter**, **Transferred**,
**Medical**, **Other**, **Adopted By Foster**, or for a placement an outcome
ended, "Ended:" and the outcome, for example "Ended: transfer out".
"(reversed)" means that outcome was undone later.

## Find which foster has an animal

Open the animal. When it is in foster, a banner at the top reads "In foster
with" the foster's name, the day, the type and the expected return day. Click
the name to open their **Fostering** tab. **Location** reads **In foster**. A
return day that has passed shows **Overdue**.

If your account can't read fosters, the banner says "a foster" and there is
no link.

## Find a foster application

1. Click **Foster Applications**.
2. Type under **Filter by applicant name...**, or use **Status**.
3. Click the **…** button on the row, then **View**.

The page shows the applicant's answers and **Status History**. You can't
change the status unless your account manages fosters.

## If something goes wrong

- **The foster isn't in the list.** They have no profile yet. Their
  application may still be waiting under **Foster Applications**.
- **Add Foster is greyed out, or a row has only View Profile.** Your account can
  read fosters but not change them.
- **Access Denied.** Your account can't read fosters. Ask an admin.
