## When to use this

Use this when a foster can't take animals for a while, has stopped fostering,
or can now take more or fewer animals. It is done on the person's **Fostering**
tab. To find the tab, see "Find a foster or a placement" in **Help**.

## What the three statuses mean

- **Active.** The foster can be offered an animal. They appear in the list
  when you place an animal, as long as they have room: fewer open
  placements than **Max Animals**.
- **Paused.** No new animal can be placed with them. Animals already with them
  stay, and they can still record care. Use it for a holiday or a break.
- **Inactive.** Off the active roster, and no new animal can be placed. A foster
  can only be made inactive when no animal is with them.

Nothing tells the foster when you change their status. Tell them yourself.

## Change the status

1. In the sidebar, click **Fosters**. At the end of the foster's row, click the
   **…** button, then **View Profile**. You can also click their name.
2. On the **Foster Status** card, click the button for what you want. An
   **Active** foster has **Pause** and **Deactivate**. A **Paused** one has
   **Resume** and **Deactivate**. An **Inactive** one has **Reactivate**.
3. A box asks, for example, "Pause this foster?" and says what it does. Click
   **Confirm**.

You should see "Foster status updated." The badge next to **Foster Status**
changes. A paused or inactive foster has no **New Placement** button on their
row or their tab, and the placement page says "Foster Not Active".

**Deactivate** is greyed out while an animal is with the foster. Return the
animal first. See
[Return an animal from foster](./return-an-animal-from-foster.md).

## Change what a foster can take

1. On the **Foster Status** card, click **Edit Capabilities**.
2. Change **Species you can foster**, **Max animals at once**, the yes and no
   answers or **Availability Notes**.
3. Click **Save**.

You should see "Foster capabilities updated." and the card shows the new
answers. Lowering **Max animals at once** doesn't remove animals already
placed. The foster just can't take another until they are under it.

Only **Max Animals** limits placements. The species and the yes and no answers
are for you to read when you choose a foster.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Cannot deactivate this foster while they have an open placement. Return
  the animal(s) first."** Return the animal, then deactivate.
- **"Select at least one species you can foster."** Keep at least one species.
- **"Max animals must be a positive whole number."** Type 1 or more.
- **"Foster profile not found."** The profile was removed. Go back to
  **Fosters**.
- **"Database Error: Failed to update foster status."** or **"Database Error:
  Failed to update foster capabilities."** Nothing was saved. Try again. If it
  keeps happening, tell an admin.
- **No buttons on the card.** Your account can read fosters but not change
  them. Ask a staff member who manages fosters.
