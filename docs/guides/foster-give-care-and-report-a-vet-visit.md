## When to use this

Use this if an animal is in foster with you. You can:

- record each dose of a medication the shelter gave you
- record a treatment the shelter scheduled, such as a dewormer
- tell the shelter about a visit to an outside vet, and add the paperwork

The shelter plans the animal's care. You record what you gave. If anything is
unclear, contact the shelter.

## Open your foster animal's care

1. In the sidebar, click **My Foster Animals**.
2. On your animal's card, click **Medical care**.

The page **Medical care for** *name* opens. It has **Alerts**, **Care due**,
**Medications**, **Vaccines and tests given**, **Your vet visit reports** and
**Documents**.

## Give a medication dose

1. Under **Medications**, find today under the medication's name. Each dose
   time (**AM**, **Midday**, **PM**) shows its state: **Due**, **Missed**, or
   what was recorded.
2. When you have given the dose, click **Give** on that time.

You should see "Dose recorded as given." The time now reads "Given 7:30 PM ·
You" (with the time you recorded it).

If the animal wouldn't take it, or you didn't give it, click the arrow next to
**Give** instead:

- **Refused…**: the animal wouldn't take it.
- **Not given…**: you didn't give it. Say why under **Why not**.
- **Record with details…**: you gave it at a different time, or want to add
  notes.

Fill in the box, then click **Record Dose**. **Missed** means a dose time has
passed with nothing recorded. You can still click **Give** on it if you gave
it late.

## Record a treatment the shelter scheduled

1. Under **Care due**, find the treatment, for example "Pyrantel pamoate ·
   Due Nov 10, 2026".
2. Click **Record as given**.
3. Check **Date given**. Add **Notes** if something happened.
4. Click **Record treatment**.

You should see "Treatment recorded as given." If the treatment repeats, it
stays under **Care due** with its next date.

Care marked "The shelter will arrange this." (a vaccine or a test, for
example) is given by the shelter, not by you.

## Report an outside vet visit

1. Under **Your vet visit reports**, click **Report outside vet visit**.
2. Check **Visit date**.
3. Under **Clinic**, pick the clinic. If it isn't listed, leave **A different
   vet** and type the vet's or clinic's name under **Vet's name**.
4. Under **What happened**, say why you went and what the vet did.
5. Click **Send report**.

You should see "Visit reported. You can now add paperwork." The report
appears with "Waiting for the shelter". Once the shelter has looked at it, it
reads "Reviewed on" and the date, or "Dismissed:" and the shelter's reason
when there was nothing to add to the record.

## Add the vet's paperwork

1. On your report, click **Add paperwork**.
2. Under **File**, pick a PDF, JPEG, PNG or WebP of up to 4 MB, such as a
   photo of the vet's notes.
3. Check **Name** (it is filled in from the file) and **Type**.
4. Click **Upload**.

You should see "Document uploaded." The file is listed on the report and
under **Documents**. Click **Add paperwork** again for each extra file.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Pick a clinic or give the vet's name."** Pick a clinic, or type a name
  under **Vet's name**.
- **"Pick a clinic or give the vet's name, rather than both."** Clear one of
  them.
- **"Say what happened at the visit."** Fill in **What happened**.
- **"The visit date can't be in the future."** Pick today or an earlier day.
- **"The visit date can't be before this animal was placed with you."** Visits
  before the animal came to you are the shelter's to record.
- **"The time given can't be in the future."** Check the time under **Record
  with details…**.
- **"Say why the dose wasn't given."** Fill in **Why not**.
- **"Upload a PDF, JPEG, PNG or WebP file."** Save the paperwork as one of
  those, or take a photo of it.
- **"This animal isn't in your care any more, so nothing can be recorded for
  it."** The foster has ended. Tell the shelter what you gave.
- **"Failed to record the dose. Please try again."** or **"Could not save the
  report. Please try again."** Nothing was saved. Try again. If it keeps
  happening, contact the shelter.
