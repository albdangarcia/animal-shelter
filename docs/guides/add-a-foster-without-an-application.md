## When to use this

Use this when someone should be able to foster now and can't wait for a
review: a walk-in, or an emergency. It creates an **Active** foster profile
at once.

For anyone who has time, ask them to apply and use
[Review a foster application](./review-a-foster-application.md) instead. It
keeps their household answers and a **Status History** on record.

## What it skips

- There is **no application**: no household questions, and no **Status
  History**. Only the foster profile exists.
- There is **no review**: the person is **Active** the moment you save.
- Nothing tells the person. If they have an account, they see "You're an
  approved foster" and **My Foster Animals** the next time they open the
  app. Tell them yourself.

## Steps

1. In the sidebar, click **Fosters**, then **Add Foster**. (**Add Foster** is
   also on **Foster Applications**. Or open a person's **Fostering** tab
   in the people directory and click **Add as Foster**, which fills in
   **Person** for you.)
2. Under **Person**, search for the person and pick them. If they aren't in the
   list and your account can edit people, pick **Add a new person**.
3. Under **Species you can foster**, pick at least one.
4. Check **Max animals at once**. It starts at 1.
5. Answer the yes and no questions you know. **Not specified** is fine. Add
   anything useful under **Availability Notes**.
6. Click **Create Foster Profile**.

You should see "Foster profile created." and the **Foster Roster** opens
with the person in the list as **Active**, for example **0/1** under
**Capacity**.

The answers are for you to read. Only **Max animals at once** limits
placements. The species and the yes and no answers aren't checked when you
place an animal.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Invalid ID format. Expected a CUID."** under **Person.** You haven't
  picked a person yet. Search and pick one.
- **"Select at least one species you can foster."** Pick a species.
- **"Max animals must be a positive whole number."** Type 1 or more.
- **"This person already has a foster profile. Manage their status from the
  existing profile instead."** Open their **Fostering** tab. See
  [Pause, deactivate or change a foster](./pause-deactivate-or-change-a-foster.md)
  to bring back one that is paused or inactive.
- **"Database Error: Failed to create foster profile."** Nothing was saved.
  Try again. If it keeps happening, tell an admin.
- **Access Denied, or the Add Foster button is greyed out.** Your account
  can't add fosters. Ask a staff member who manages fosters.
