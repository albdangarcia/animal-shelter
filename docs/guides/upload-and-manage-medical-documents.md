## When to use this

Use this to keep an animal's medical paperwork with its record: lab results,
vet records, imaging, prescriptions and consent forms. Each file opens in a
new tab. Paperwork a foster sent with a vet visit report shows here too, with
"Sent with a foster's report of the visit on" the day.

## Upload a document

1. Open the animal, then the **Medical** tab, then **Documents**.
2. Click **Upload document**.
3. Under **File**, pick a PDF, JPEG, PNG or WebP of up to 4 MB.
4. Check **Name**. It is filled in from the file name; change it to say what
   the paper is, for example "Ear cytology".
5. Under **Type**, pick **Lab result**, **Vet record**, **Imaging**,
   **Prescription**, **Consent form** or **Other**.
6. Pick the **Document date** if the paper has a day on it.
7. Under **Belongs to**, pick the entry it goes with, such as the exam or
   vaccine, or leave **No entry**.
8. Click **Upload**.

You should see "Document uploaded." It is listed first, with who uploaded it,
the file type and size, and "Belongs to:" the entry. Click its name to open
it. To see one kind only, pick it under **Type** above the list.

## Correct a document's details

1. Find the document and click its **…** menu, then **Edit**.
2. Change the **Name**, **Type**, **Document date**, **Belongs to** or
   **Notes**.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

You should see "Document updated." The file itself can't change: if it is
the wrong file, void it and upload the right one.

## Void a document

Void a document that should never have been uploaded: the wrong animal or the
wrong file.

1. Click its **…** menu, then **Void…**.
2. Type why, for example "Uploaded to the wrong animal".
3. Click **Void**.

You should see "Document voided." It stays in the list, struck through,
marked **Voided** with who voided it and why, and keeps its file. A void
can't be undone: if it was right after all, upload it again.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Give the document a name."** Type a **Name**.
- **"Pick the document type."** Pick a **Type**.
- **"Upload a PDF, JPEG, PNG or WebP file."** Save the paperwork as one of
  those, or take a photo of it.
- **"This file is larger than 4 MB."** Scan it at a lower resolution, or
  split it into parts.
- **"That entry was voided, so no document can be linked to it."** Someone
  voided the entry while you were filling in the form. Pick another entry
  under **Belongs to**, or **No entry**.
- **The Void button is greyed out.** Type a reason first.
- **A voided document holds someone's private details.** Only an admin can
  remove its file. Ask them.
- **No Upload document button.** Uploading needs a staff member who manages
  medical care. Ask them.
