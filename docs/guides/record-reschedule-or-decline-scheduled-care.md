## When to use this

Use this when an animal has care under **Due** on its **Medical** tab, and
you want to settle one row: it was given, it moves to another day, or it
won't be given. Care added at intake by a protocol shows "From the Puppy
protocol" (or the protocol's name), and is handled the same way.

To work through care across the whole shelter, use the Due page instead:
[Schedule care and read the Due page](./schedule-care-and-read-the-due-page.md).

## Find the care

1. Open the animal, then the **Medical** tab. **Overview** opens.
2. Under **Due**, each row shows the item, its kind, and when it is due. A row
   that is late or due today has an **Overdue** or **Due today** badge. See
   the [glossary](./medical-status-glossary.md#overdue).
3. Each row has up to three buttons: **Record**, **Reschedule** and
   **Decline**. Which ones you see depends on what your account can do.

## Record it as given

1. On the row, click **Record**.
2. A form opens, titled with the item, for example **Record DHPP as given**.
   It says when the care was due, and the item's usual details are filled in.
3. Fill in the form for its kind, then click its **Record** button:
   - a vaccine, test or treatment: see
     [Record a vaccine, test or treatment](./record-a-vaccine-test-or-treatment.md)
   - a surgery, such as a spay or neuter: see
     [Record a surgery, spay/neuter or exemption](./record-a-surgery-spay-neuter-or-exemption.md)
   - an exam: see [Record an exam](./record-an-exam.md)
   - a microchip: see [Record or correct a microchip](./record-or-correct-a-microchip.md)

You should see "Entry recorded." (or "Recorded. Next DHPP due …" when the
next dose is planned). The row leaves **Due** and appears under **Recent
entries**.

## Reschedule it

1. On the row, click **Reschedule**.
2. The box says the day it is due now. Under **Due date**, pick the new day.
   Days before today are greyed out: pick today or a later day.
3. Click **Reschedule**.

You should see "Entry rescheduled." The row shows its new date, and **Show 1
change** under it lists the old and new days.

## Decline it

Decline when the care won't be given at all, for example because it was done
somewhere else or the vet advised against it.

1. On the row, click **Decline**. The box that opens says a decline is final.
2. Type why in the box (**Why won't it be given?**), for example "Ran at the
   referral clinic instead".
3. Click **Decline Entry**.

You should see "Entry declined." The row leaves **Due**. On **History** the
entry stays, marked **Declined**, with "Reason declined:" and your reason.

Declining a spay or neuter is different: it records an exemption, with its
reason. See
[Record a surgery, spay/neuter or exemption](./record-a-surgery-spay-neuter-or-exemption.md).

## If something goes wrong

- **"That is already its due date. Pick another day."** You picked the day it
  is already due.
- **Only Decline shows on the row.** The animal has left the shelter's care
  (it has an outcome), so its care can't be given or moved. Decline it, or
  ask staff who manage medical care to void it.
- **The Decline Entry button is greyed out.** Type a reason first.
- **No Record button, but the animal is in care.** Recording care as given
  needs an account that can record medical care. Ask an admin.
- **No Reschedule button.** Moving care to another day needs a staff member
  who manages medical care. Ask them. The same goes for declining a spay or
  neuter.
- **Declined by mistake.** A decline can't be undone. Staff who manage medical
  care can void the entry and schedule it again.
- **"Access Denied. You do not have permission to perform this action."** Your
  account can't record medical care. Ask an admin.
