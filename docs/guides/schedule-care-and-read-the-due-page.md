## When to use this

Schedule care when an animal needs a vaccine, test, treatment, surgery,
microchip or exam on a later day. Use the Due page each day to see what is
overdue, due today or due in the next 7 days, and to record, move or decline
it.

If the care has already been given, record it instead:
[Record a vaccine, test or treatment](./record-a-vaccine-test-or-treatment.md).

## Schedule care for one animal

1. Open the animal, then the **Medical** tab.
2. Click **Add entry**, then **Schedule…**.
3. Under **Kind**, pick what it is, for example **Vaccine**.
4. Under **Item**, pick the item. An item marked **(already scheduled)** is
   on the animal's due list already; check before adding a second one.
5. Pick the **Due date**. It must be today or later.
6. Add **Notes** if whoever gives it needs to know something.
7. Click **Schedule**.

You should see "Entry scheduled." The care appears under **Due** on the
animal's **Overview**, and on the Due page once it is 7 days away or less.

## Read the Due page

1. In the sidebar, open **Medical**, then **Due**.
2. The page lists care that is overdue, due today or due in the next 7 days.
   It is grouped by where each animal is: its **Location**, **In foster
   with** a foster, or **Not housed**.
3. Within each place, rows are split into **Overdue**, **Today** and
   **Upcoming**. An overdue row shows how late it is, for example "3 days
   overdue". See the [glossary](./medical-status-glossary.md#overdue).
4. To see only some kinds, click **Kind** and tick them, for example
   **Treatment**.
5. A **Spay/neuter exemption review** row means an animal's exemption is up
   for review. Click **Schedule spay/neuter** or **Record a new exemption**.
6. **Foster reports** at the bottom lists outside vet visits fosters have
   reported that the shelter hasn't reviewed yet.

## Work through the rows

On each row:

- **Record**: the care was given. The same form opens as on the animal's
  page; see
  [Record a vaccine, test or treatment](./record-a-vaccine-test-or-treatment.md).
- **Reschedule**: pick a new **Due date** and click **Reschedule**. You should
  see "Entry rescheduled." If the new day is more than 7 days away, the row
  leaves the page until then.
- **Decline**: the care won't be given. Type why in the box (**Why won't it be
  given?**), then click **Decline Entry**. You should see "Entry declined."
  The row leaves the due list and stays on the animal's record as declined. A
  decline is final. Declining a spay or neuter records an exemption instead,
  with its reason.

## Record several animals at once

1. Tick the box on each row you gave. They must all be the same vaccine or
   treatment.
2. Click **Record selected as given**. It shows how many you picked.
3. Check **Date given**, **Given** and the dose details. They are recorded the
   same for every animal.
4. Leave **Schedule the next one** ticked to plan each animal's next dose.
5. Click **Record 2 as given** (the number is how many you picked).

You should see "Recorded 2 entries as given." followed by what happened to
the next doses: "Next dose scheduled for 2." when each animal gets a new one,
or "2 already had one planned." when the next dose was planned already (for
example by a protocol). Those animals keep the dose they had; no second one
is added.

## If something goes wrong

- **"The due date is required."** or **"Pick an item."** Fill in every field
  marked with *.
- **"The due date can't be in the past."** Pick today or a later day. Care
  that is already late is recorded, not scheduled.
- **"That is already its due date. Pick another day."** You picked the day it
  is already due.
- **"This animal has left the shelter's care, so nothing can be scheduled for
  it."** The animal has an outcome, so no new care can be planned for it.
- **Record selected as given is greyed out.** The message beside it says why:
  - "The selected entries are for different items (…). Select entries for
    one item."
  - "Tests are recorded one at a time, since each animal has its own
    results." Record each test from its own row.
  - "At most … entries can be recorded at once." Record the rest in a second
    round.
- **No Schedule…, Reschedule or Schedule spay/neuter.** Planning care needs a
  staff member who manages medical care. Ask them.
- **Declined by mistake.** A decline can't be undone. Staff who manage
  medical care can void the entry and schedule it again.
