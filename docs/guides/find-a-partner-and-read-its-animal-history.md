## When to use this

Use this to find an outside organisation the shelter works with, a rescue, a
vet clinic, another shelter or a government agency such as animal control, to
see who to call there, and to see which animals came from it or went to it. A
read-only account can do everything here. To add or change a partner you need
an account that can edit partners, so ask a staff member.

## Find a partner

1. In the sidebar, click **Partner Directory**. The partners are listed by
   name. Each row shows **Name**, **Type**, **Status**, **Contacts** (how many
   people are linked), **Email**, **City** and **State**.
2. Type in **Filter by name, email, or city...**. The list shows partners
   whose name, email or city contains what you typed. A phone number isn't
   searched.
3. To see one kind of partner, click **Type** and tick **Shelter**, **Rescue
   Group**, **Vet Clinic** or **Government Agency**. To see only partners that
   are in use, or only retired ones, click **Status** and tick **Active** or
   **Inactive**. A **Reset** button appears once you've filtered. Click it to
   see them all again.
4. To sort, click a column's name, then **Asc** or **Desc**. **Customize
   Columns** hides columns, and **Rows per page** shows more.
5. Click the partner's name to open it.

If nothing matches, the list says "No results." Try less of the name.

## Read a partner

The top of the page shows the name, the type, **Active** or **Inactive**, the
**Email**, **Phone**, **Website** and **Address**, and under **Primary
Contact** the person to call, with their role, email and phone. Click their
name to open their record in the People Directory. A **Quick Note** appears
if staff pinned one. Four tiles count **Contacts**, **Transfers In**,
**Transfers Out** and **Notes**.

Three tabs sit under it:

- **Animal History**: every animal the partner sent to the shelter, tagged
  **Received From** with the intake type, and every animal the shelter sent
  to it, tagged **Transferred To** with the outcome type. Click an animal's
  name to open it. A transfer out that was later reversed stays in the list,
  marked. The first ten show. A button such as **Show 5 More** shows the rest.
- **Contacts**: the people at the partner, with their role, email and phone.
  The primary contact is first. Staff add and change them.
- **Notes**: dated notes about the partner, newest first. Staff write them.

## If something goes wrong

- **Access Denied**. Your account can't read partners. Ask an admin.
- **Partner Not Found**. The link is wrong. Go back to **Partner Directory**.
- **Add Partner** and **Edit Partner** are greyed out, and the rows have no
  **…** button. Your account can read partners but not change them. Ask a
  staff member.
- A partner is missing. Check the **Type** and **Status** filters. If it still
  isn't there, ask a staff member to add it.
