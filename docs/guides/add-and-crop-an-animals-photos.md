## When to use this

Use this to add photos to an animal. Each photo is cropped to a square
before it is saved, and you check how it will look on the website as you
crop.

To fix a photo already saved, see
[Replace or reframe an animal's photo](./replace-or-reframe-an-animals-photo.md).
To pick the photo shown first, or delete one, see
[Reorder or delete an animal's photos](./reorder-or-delete-an-animals-photos.md).

The site shows a photo in several shapes (a circle on the home page, wide
frames on the listing and the animal's page, small squares), and each one
cuts off what doesn't fit, so crop with the animal in the middle. Only the
square you choose is saved, not the whole photo: keep the original on your
phone or computer.

## Steps

1. Open the animal, then the **Photos** tab.
2. Under **Upload New Images**, click **Choose photos** and pick one or more
   photos, or drag the files onto the box. Photos can be JPEG, PNG or WebP.
3. A **Crop photos** box opens on the first photo, with a square drawn over
   it. Move the square over the animal:
   - drag the square to move it;
   - drag its corner to make it bigger or smaller, or use the **Zoom**
     slider (to the right zooms in) or your mouse's scroll wheel;
   - on a phone, drag the square, and use the **Zoom** slider to resize it.
4. Check the previews under **How it will look**: **Home page**, **Listing**,
   **Animal's page** and **Square**. If the animal's head or feet are cut off
   in the **Home page** or **Square** preview, move the square or zoom out.
5. The **Listing** and **Animal's page** previews are wider than the square,
   so its top or bottom is cut off there. If the animal's head (or feet) is
   cut off, drag either preview down (or up) until the animal is in frame;
   both move together. **Centre** puts the photo back in the middle.
6. Click **Save photo**.

You should see "Photo saved." with the photo's file name at the top of the
screen, and the photo appears last under **Current Images**. The animal's
activity feed shows that you uploaded a new photo. To make it the **Primary
photo**, the one shown first, drag it to the first place.

## Add several photos at once

If you picked several photos, the next one opens after each save. The list
beside the photo (above it on a phone) shows them all: a tick marks the ones
saved, and clicking one opens it. To leave a photo out, click the **×**
beside it, and to add more to the list, click **Add more photos**. The box
closes after the last photo is saved.

Closing the box with **×** or Esc while some photos aren't saved asks
"Discard the photos you haven't saved?". Click **Keep cropping** to go back,
or **Discard** to close without them. Photos already saved stay.

## With a keyboard

The square is ready for the keys when a photo opens (its outline turns
dashed; if it isn't, click the square or press Tab until it is). Use the
arrow keys to move it (hold Shift to move further) and **+** or **-** to
resize it. **J** and **K** open the next and previous photo, and Enter
saves.

To frame the wide previews, press Tab until the **Listing** or **Animal's
page** preview has an outline, then use the up and down arrow keys (Home and
End go to the top and the bottom).

## If something goes wrong

- **"This file can't be opened as a photo in this browser, so it wasn't
  added. Add a JPEG, PNG or WebP photo. To have an iPhone take JPEG photos,
  set Settings > Camera > Formats to Most Compatible."** The message names
  the file. It isn't a photo, or it is one your browser can't show (an
  iPhone's HEIC photo outside Safari, say), so there is nothing to crop. Any
  other photos you picked with it still open. Save a copy of the photo as a
  JPEG and add that.
- **"Failed to upload image. Please try again."** The photo wasn't added and
  is still open in the box: click **Save photo** again. If it keeps failing,
  tell an admin.
- **"Couldn't reach the server, so the image wasn't uploaded. Check your
  connection and try again."** Check that you are online, then click **Save
  photo** again. The photo is still open in the box.
- **"The cropped photo couldn't be made, so it wasn't uploaded. Try again,
  or close other tabs first."** Your browser ran out of memory making the
  square. Close other tabs and click **Save photo** again.
- **"This animal wasn't found, so the photo wasn't added. Reload the page
  and try again."** Reload the page. If the animal is still there, add the
  photo again; if it keeps happening, tell an admin.
- **"The photo's framing wasn't understood. Reload the page and try
  again."** Reload the page and add the photo again. If it keeps happening,
  tell an admin.
- **"This photo is too big to upload. Photos can be up to 4 MB. Save a
  smaller copy as a JPEG and add that instead."**, **"This file isn't a
  photo. Add a JPEG, PNG or WebP image."** or **"HEIC photos can't be shown
  in most browsers…"** A photo cropped in the **Crop photos** box is a
  small WebP, so you should not see these. If you do, tell an admin.
- **"Unauthorized: You must be logged in."** Your sign-in has run out.
  Reload the page, sign in, and add the photo again.
- **"Your account is not associated with a person record."** Your account
  isn't set up to record changes. Tell an admin.
