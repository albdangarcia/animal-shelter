## When to use this

Use this when someone gives you their application in person or by phone and
they haven't filed it themselves. You type their answers in, and the
application goes into the same list as the ones people file on the website.
It starts as **Pending**.

You enter an application from the person's record, so they must be in the
directory first. Applicants who apply on the website don't need this. To fix
a mistake in an application you already entered, see
[Edit an adoption application for an applicant](./edit-an-adoption-application-for-an-applicant.md).
What each status means is in
[the glossary](./adoption-application-status-glossary.md).

## Steps

1. In the sidebar, open **People Directory** and click the person. If they
   aren't there, add them first.
2. Click the **Adoption Applications** tab, then **Add Application**.
   The page **New Adoption Application** opens, saying "Submitting on behalf
   of" the person.
3. Under **Animal to Adopt**, click **Search for an animal...** and type at
   least two letters of the animal's name. Click the animal in the list. Only
   animals that are **Published** are listed (up to ten), so an animal that
   is **Pending Adoption** or has already left won't appear, and neither will
   one this person already has an active application for. Several animals can
   share a name: the list shows only the name and species. To pick a
   different animal, click **Clear**.
4. Check **Applicant Information**. It is filled in from the person's record.
   Change anything they tell you is different. **Address Line 2** is
   optional.
5. Fill in **Home & Lifestyle**: **Living Situation**, **Household Size**
   (including the applicant), whether they have a yard, whether they have
   landlord permission (needed when they rent) and whether there are children
   in the home. When there are, list their ages under **Children's Ages**,
   separated by commas. **Other Animals** is optional. These answers are
   filled in from the person's household profile when there is one.
6. Fill in **Experience & Intent**: **Animal Experience** and **Reason for
   Adoption**, in the applicant's own words as far as you can.
7. Click **Submit Application**.

You should see "Application submitted successfully." and the person's
**Adoption Applications** tab opens with the application on top, reading
**Pending**. Open it with **Review**: its **Status History** says "Application
submitted by staff on behalf of applicant." and the answers carry a note that
they were transcribed rather than typed by the applicant.

## What is saved, and what isn't

- The application itself, with a snapshot of the answers as you typed them.
- The same household answers are saved to the person's household profile
  (**Edit Household Info** on their record), so the next application starts
  with them.
- The person's contact details (**Edit Contact Info**) are **not** changed.
  If the phone or address has changed, update their record too.

If the person has an account, the application also appears on their own
**My Adoption Applications** page. They can correct it there while it is
**Pending**, and they see anything you change.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Invalid ID format. Expected a CUID."** under **Animal to Adopt**. You
  didn't pick an animal. Search for one and click it.
- **Required field messages**, for example "Reason for adoption is required",
  "Applicant phone is required", "Invalid ZIP code" (it takes five digits),
  "Please provide the ages of the children if 'Yes' is selected." or
  "Landlord permission is required when renting." Fix the field and submit
  again.
- **"An active application already exists for this person and animal."** The
  person already has an application for this animal that is still **Pending**,
  **Reviewing**, **Waitlisted**, **Approved** or **Adopted**. Open it from
  their tab. You can enter a new one after a **Rejected** or **Withdrawn**
  application.
- **"This animal is not available for adoption applications."** The animal
  is no longer **Published**, for example because another application was just
  approved. Pick another animal.
- **"Type at least 2 characters to search."** or **"No published animals
  found."** Type more of the name, or check that the animal is published on
  its record.
- **"Person not found."** The record was removed. Go back to **People
  Directory**.
- **Page Not Found** on **New Adoption Application**. The page only opens from
  a person's tab. Start from **Add Application** there.
- **Access Denied.** Your account can't enter applications. Ask a staff
  member who can.
- **"Database Error: Failed to create adoption application."** Nothing was
  saved. Try again; if it keeps happening, tell an admin.
