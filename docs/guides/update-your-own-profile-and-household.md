## When to use this

Use this when your name, phone, address or email changes, or when your home
does: you move, you get a yard, or you have a child. The shelter uses your
details to contact you. It also fills your household answers in on an adoption
or foster application, so you don't type them again. This is your own page: no
one else's.

## Change your contact details

1. At the bottom of the sidebar, click your name and email, then **Account**.
   On the shelter's public website, click your picture, then **Account**. The
   page **My Profile** opens.
2. Under **Contact Information**, change what you need: **Name**, **Email**,
   **Phone**, **Street Address**, **City**, **State** or **Zip Code**. Only
   **Name** is required. Type your phone the way you usually write it.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

You should see "Profile updated successfully." You stay on the page, and your
new name shows at the bottom of the sidebar.

Your **Email** is also the address you sign in with. If you change it, sign in
with the new address from then on. If you leave **Email** empty, you still
sign in with the address you had, but the shelter has no email for you on
your record.

## Change your household answers

1. On the same page, scroll down to **Household & Lifestyle**.
2. Answer or change each question:
   - **Living Situation**: **Own Home**, **Rent Apartment**, **Rent House**,
     **Live With Family** or **Other**.
   - **Household Size**: everyone who lives in your home, including you.
   - **Do you have a yard?**
   - **Do you have landlord permission?** It shows only if you rent.
   - **Do you have children at home?** If you pick **Yes**, type their ages
     under **Ages of children**, with commas, such as `5, 8, 12`.
   - **Other Animals in the Home**: any pets you have now. This one is
     optional.
   - **Experience with Animals**.
3. Click **Save Household Info**.

You should see "Household information updated successfully."

## What the shelter does with them

- When you apply to adopt or to foster, the form starts with these answers.
  Check them on the form, and change anything that isn't true now.
- When you send an application, the household answers on it are saved here
  too, so this page holds the most recent ones. Applying to adopt or to
  foster also saves the name, phone and address you typed on the application
  to your profile above. Editing an application you already sent does not
  change this page.
- An application you already sent keeps the answers you gave then. Changing
  this page later doesn't change it. To change an application, see "Check,
  change, withdraw or reactivate your adoption application" in **Help**.
- Shelter staff can read these answers, and can correct them for you if you
  ask. Whoever saves last decides what is kept.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Name is required."** Type your **Name**.
- **"Please enter a valid email address."** Check your **Email**.
- **"A person with this email already exists."** Another person on the shelter's
  list has that email. Use a different one, or ask the shelter.
- **"This email is already the sign-in address for another account."** Someone
  else signs in with that address. Use a different one.
- **"Missing or invalid fields. Failed to update profile."** Read the red
  messages under the fields and fix them.
- **"Living situation is required."**, **"Yard information is required."**,
  **"Children information is required."** or **"Animal experience is
  required"**. Answer the question.
- **"Landlord permission is required when renting."** Answer the landlord
  question.
- **"Please provide the ages of the children if 'Yes' is selected."** Type the
  ages, or pick **No**.
- **"Household size is required."** or **"Household size must be a number."**
  Type how many people live in your home.
- **"Household size must be between 1 and 50."** Type a number in that range.
- **"Database Error: Failed to update profile."** or **"Database Error: Failed
  to update household profile."** Nothing was saved. Try again. If it keeps
  happening, ask the shelter.
- **Access Denied.** Your account can't use this page. Ask the shelter.
