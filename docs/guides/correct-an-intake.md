## When to use this

Use this when an intake was recorded with a detail typed wrong: the day, the
type, who surrendered the animal, which partner sent it, where a stray was
found, or the notes. The intake is corrected in place, and the animal's
**Activity** keeps a line saying what changed and what it was before.

You can't change which animal the intake is for, or who recorded it.

## Steps

1. In the sidebar, open **Intakes**.
2. Type the animal's name in **Filter by animal name...**. An animal that
   came back has one row per intake: check the **Intake Date** to pick the
   right one.
3. Click the **…** button at the end of the row, then **Edit…**. The page
   **Edit Intake** opens, saying which animal it is for and who recorded it.
4. Change what is wrong:
   - **Intake Type**. The new type asks for its own source: the **Source
     Partner** for **Transfer In**, the **Surrendering Person** for **Owner
     Surrender**, or the **City**, **State** and **Address / Cross Streets**
     for **Stray**. The old type's source is cleared when you save.
   - **Intake Date**. Days after today can't be picked.
   - **Internal Notes**, or the source fields of the current type.
5. Click **Update Intake**.

You should see "Intake updated successfully." The **Intakes** list opens with
the change. If you changed nothing, you see "No changes to save." instead.

To see the record of the change, open the animal. Its **Activity** shows
"Olivia Chen corrected an intake" (with your name). Click **Show details**
for what changed, for example "Intake was corrected: the date changed from
Sep 8, 2026 to Nov 1, 2026." When a type change clears a source, it says
what was there, for example "the source partner was cleared (was City Animal
Control)".

## If something goes wrong

- **"The intake date can't be after this stay's outcome on Nov 3, 2026."**
  (with the outcome's day) The animal left on that day, so it must have
  arrived on it or before. Pick that day or an earlier one, or check the
  outcome's day.
- **"The intake date can't be before the previous outcome on Nov 3, 2026."**
  This is a return. The animal came back after it last left, so pick that
  day or a later one.
- **"The intake date can't be Nov 14, 2026: this animal already has an
  intake and an outcome that day, and a day can hold only one of each."**
  (with the day; it may say "an intake" or "an outcome" alone) Pick another
  day.
- **"The intake date can't be after the foster placement with … began on
  …"** (with the foster's name and day) The animal went to a foster from
  this stay, so it arrived on that day or before. Pick that day or an
  earlier one.
- **"Source partner is required for transfers."**, **"A surrendering person
  is required."**, **"City is required for strays."**, **"State is required
  for strays."** or **"Address is required for strays."** Fill in the source
  the **Intake Type** asks for.
- **The intake is on the wrong animal.** The animal can't be changed here,
  and an intake can't be removed. Tell an admin.
- **No … button on the rows, or Access Denied on Edit Intake.** Your account
  can read intakes but not correct them. Ask a staff member who takes animals
  in.
- **"Database Error: Failed to update intake."** Nothing was saved. Try
  again; if it keeps happening, tell an admin.
