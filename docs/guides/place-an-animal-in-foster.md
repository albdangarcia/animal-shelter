## When to use this

Use this when an animal goes home with a foster. The foster must already be
on the roster: see
[Review a foster application](./review-a-foster-application.md) or
[Add a foster without an application](./add-a-foster-without-an-application.md).
A placement is the animal actually staying with them, separate from the
foster's profile.

When the animal comes back, see
[Return an animal from foster](./return-an-animal-from-foster.md). For a
**Foster To Adopt** placement, see also
[Turn a foster into an adoption](./turn-a-foster-into-an-adoption.md).

## Steps

1. Start from whichever you know first:
   - the animal: open it and click **Place in Foster**, below **Create
     Outcome**.
   - the foster: click **Fosters**, click the **…** button on their row, then
     **New Placement**. You can also click **New Placement** on their
     **Fostering** tab.

   The page **Place Animal With Foster** opens.
2. Pick what you don't know yet. From a foster, pick the **Animal**. From an
   animal, pick the **Foster**. Each foster shows their open animals out of
   their maximum, for example "Rosa Lindqvist · 1/2".
3. Under **Placement Type**, pick one. See the next section.
4. If you know when the animal should be back, pick an **Expected Return
   Date**. It can't be in the past, and can stay empty.
5. Add any **Notes**.
6. Click **Place in Foster**.

You should see "Placement created." and the animal's page opens with "In
foster with" the foster's name, the day and the type. **Location** reads **In
foster**: the animal leaves its unit. **Activity** shows "placed this animal
with a foster", with your notes.

The foster now sees the animal under **My Foster Animals**.

## The four placement types

- **General**, **Medical** and **Long Term** only label the placement, on the
  animal's banner and in the foster's history. Nothing else changes.
- **Foster To Adopt** also moves the animal's listing to **Pending Adoption**
  for as long as the placement is open. Returning the animal puts the listing
  back as it was. To finish the adoption, see
  [Turn a foster into an adoption](./turn-a-foster-into-an-adoption.md).

If the foster is also adopting the animal, approve their adoption application
**before** you place it as **Foster To Adopt**. Approving needs the animal to be
**Published**, and this type moves it to **Pending Adoption**. Once it is
pending, approving fails with "This animal is no longer available for
adoption."

An expected return date that has passed shows an **Overdue** badge on the
banner. The foster sees "(date passed)" and nothing more.

## Who and what you can pick

The foster list shows only **Active** fosters with room. A paused or inactive
foster, or one at their maximum, is not offered. Only animals in the
shelter's care that are not already in foster are offered.

## If something goes wrong

- **Foster Not Active** (page). The foster is paused or inactive. See
  [Pause, deactivate or change a foster](./pause-deactivate-or-change-a-foster.md).
- **At Capacity** (page). The foster has as many animals as their maximum.
  Return one or raise **Max Animals**.
- **Already In Foster** (page). Return the animal from its current foster first.
- **Not In Care** (page). The animal has left the shelter, or isn't here yet.
- **"Invalid ID format. Expected a CUID."** under **Animal** or **Foster.**
  Pick one.
- **"A placement type is required."** Pick a **Placement Type**.
- **"An expected return date cannot be in the past."** Pick today or later.
- **"This foster is already at capacity."**, **"Cannot place an animal with
  this foster: their profile status is paused."**, **"This animal already has an
  open foster placement."** or **"This animal is not currently in the
  shelter's care."** Someone changed the animal or the foster after you opened
  the page. Reload and look again.
- **"This animal has already left the shelter, so it can't be placed in
  foster."** An outcome was recorded. See "Take back an animal that left" in
  **Help**.
- **"This animal or foster changed while the placement was being made. Please
  try again."** Nothing was saved. Click **Place in Foster** again.
- **"Database Error: Failed to create foster placement."** Nothing was saved.
  Try again. If it keeps happening, tell an admin.
- **Access Denied.** Your account can't place animals. Ask a staff member who
  manages fosters.
