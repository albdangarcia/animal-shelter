## When to use this

Use this only when a login is linked to the record of someone it doesn't
belong to. It happens when a person signs up and the app links their login to
an existing record because the email matches, but the shelter had typed that
email on the wrong person's record. The wrong person then sees those
applications under their own **My Adoption Applications**, and the right person
can't be helped.

Don't use it:

- When a person can't get into their own account. Unlinking restores nobody's
  access. Staff can edit the record for them without unlinking anything.
- To turn a login off. Role management does that.
- To merge two records of one person. The app can't merge people.

**There is no way to put the link back.** Nothing in the app undoes it. Check
the record before you click.

## Before you start

1. In the sidebar, click **People Directory** and click the person. Staff can
   open it too, but only an admin sees the **Unlink Account** button.
2. On the **Profile** tab, under **Account Information**, read **Sign-in
   Email**. Check that this login really belongs to someone other than the
   person named at the top. Look at the **Adoption Applications** and
   **Notes** tabs if you are unsure.

## Steps

1. Under **Account Information**, click **Unlink Account**. A box asks
   "Unlink this login from NAME's record?" and names the login's email.
2. Read the box. It says, in short, to do this only when the login belongs to
   someone other than NAME; that it does not help someone who has lost access
   to their own account; that the login keeps working and gets a new, empty
   person record; that this record keeps its applications, notes and history
   and becomes staff-editable again; that both records are noted; and "There
   is no way to put the link back from here."
3. Click **Unlink Account** in the box, or **Cancel** to stop.

You should see "Login account unlinked. It now has its own person record, and
this one is staff-editable again." and the new record opens.

## What moves and what stays

**The login moves.** It keeps working with the same email and password, but it
now belongs to a new person record:

- The new record has only the **Name** the login was created with, which may
  not be spelled right, and the login's email. It has no phone, address,
  household, applications or history. Whoever signs in with that login now
  sees an empty **My Adoption Applications** and an empty profile.
- The new record's **Notes** tab has a note, with your name as the author,
  saying how it was made.

**Everything else stays on the old record**: its address, phone, household,
applications, notes and animal history. It becomes a **Contact Record** with
**Login Account: No**, so staff can edit its email again. Its **Notes** tab
has a note, written by you, that says the login was moved.

**The email.** If the old record's email was the same as the login's, it is
removed from the old record and goes with the login. The note says so. This
stops the next sign-up with that address linking to the old record again. If
the two emails differed, neither is changed. If the login's email is already
on a third record, the new record starts with no email and its note says so.

## After you unlink

1. On the new record, which opens for you, correct the **Name**, and add what
   you know under **Edit Contact Info**. You can't change its email: the
   login signs in with it, and only that person can change it from their own
   profile.
2. On the old record, add the right person's own email if you know it, under
   **Edit Contact Info**.

## If something goes wrong

- **"This person has no login account, so there is nothing to unlink."**
  Someone already unlinked it, or the page is out of date. Reload it.
- **"Person not found."** The record was removed. Go back to **People
  Directory**.
- **"You cannot unlink your own account from your own record."** You can't
  unlink the login you are signed in with. Ask another admin.
- **"Database Error: Failed to unlink the login account."** Nothing changed.
  Try again. If it keeps happening, tell whoever looks after the app.
- **Unlinked the wrong login.** Nothing in the app can undo it. Tell whoever
  looks after the app's data, and don't create more records for that person
  meanwhile.
- **No Unlink Account button.** Only an admin can unlink a login. Ask one.
