## When to use this

Use this to fix the answers on an application: a phone number, an address, a
household answer or the reason for adopting. It is most useful for an
application you typed in for someone, because the answers were heard
and transcribed, and a mishearing is likelier there than in one the
applicant typed. See
[Enter an adoption application for someone](./enter-an-adoption-application-for-someone.md).

You can edit an application that is **Pending**, **Reviewing**,
**Waitlisted** or **Approved**, whoever entered it. You can't change which
animal it is for, or its status. For a different animal, enter a new
application. To change the status, see "Review an application and change its
status" in **Help**. The statuses are explained in
[the glossary](./adoption-application-status-glossary.md).

## Steps

1. Open the application's editor in either of two ways:
   - In **People Directory**, open the person, click their **Adoption
     Applications** tab, click the **…** button on the application's row,
     then **Edit**.
   - Or open the application with **Review** and click **Edit Application
     Fields**.
2. The page **Edit Adoption Application** opens, saying "Editing application
   for" the animal. Change the fields that are wrong. Required fields are
   marked with a star.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

You should see "Application updated successfully." You are taken back to the
person's tab, or to the review page when you started there. Open the
application with **Review**: **Edited after submission** names you and the
time. Status History does not get a line for an edit.

## What the applicant sees

If the person has an account, their application page shows **Changed by the
shelter**, with your name and the time, and asks them to contact the shelter if
it isn't what they meant. That is why you should only change what they told
you.

## Who can correct what

- **Staff** can edit the answers while the application is **Pending**,
  **Reviewing**, **Waitlisted** or **Approved**, whether the person has an
  account or not. Once it is **Rejected**, **Withdrawn**, **Adopted** or
  **Closed**, nobody can edit it.
- **The applicant** can edit their own application only while it is
  **Pending**. After staff pick it up, they have to ask the shelter to change
  it. An application they withdraw and then reactivate is **Pending** again,
  so they can edit it again.
- Neither of you changes the person's contact record (**Edit Contact Info**
  on their record). Staff edits do save the household answers to the person's
  household profile.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Missing or Invalid Fields. Failed to Update Application."** Look for the
  message under each field, for example "Invalid ZIP code" (five digits),
  "Reason for adoption is required" or "Landlord permission is required when
  renting." Fix them and save again.
- **"Cannot edit rejected applications."** (it may say another status) The
  application is settled, so its answers can't change.
- **"This application has moved to a status staff cannot edit."** Someone
  rejected it, or an outcome closed it, while you were editing. Open it with
  **Review** to see.
- **"Application not found."** Go back to the person's tab and open it again.
- **No Edit option.** The application is **Rejected**, **Withdrawn**,
  **Adopted** or **Closed**.
- **Access Denied.** Your account can't edit applications. Ask a staff member
  who can.
- **"Database Error: Failed to update application."** Nothing was saved. Try
  again; if it keeps happening, tell an admin.
