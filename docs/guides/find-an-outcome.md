## When to use this

Use the **Outcomes** list to see every time an animal left the shelter:
adoptions, transfers out, returns to owner, returns to field, animals lost
in care, deaths and the rest. Each
departure is one row, so an animal that left more than once has a row for
each. Outcomes that were reversed stay on the list, marked **Reversed**.

## Steps

1. In the sidebar, open **Outcomes**. The newest outcomes are at the top.
2. To find one, type in **Filter by animal, applicant, owner, staff, or
   partner name...**. It matches the animal, the adopter, the owner, the
   partner, or the staff member who recorded it.
3. To see one kind of outcome, click **Type** and tick **Adoption**,
   **Transfer Out**, **Return To Owner** or another type. Tick more than one
   to see them together. Press Esc to close it.
4. To see the oldest first, change **Newest First** to **Oldest First**.
5. Once a name or a type is set, a **Reset** button appears. Click it to see
   the whole list again.

Each row shows:

- **Animal**: its name. Click it to open the animal.
- **Recipient**: who took the animal, with a label: **Adopter**, **Partner**
  or **Owner**. "N/A" for a death, a euthanasia, **Return To Field**, **Lost
  In Care** or **Other Live Outcome**.
- **Type**: how it left. **Reversed** beside it means the outcome was voided
  by an admin; point at it to see who reversed it, when, and why.
- **Outcome Date**: the day the animal left.
- **Processed By**: the staff member who recorded the outcome.

Use **Customize Columns** to hide columns, and **Rows per page** to show more
at once.

## What the … button offers

What the **…** button at the end of a row offers depends on your account:

- **Edit**, to correct the outcome, for staff who record outcomes.
- **Reverse…**, for admins.
- **View reversal**, on a reversed outcome, for everyone: who reversed it,
  when and why.

Without any of these, a row has no **…** button.

## If something goes wrong

- **No Outcomes in the sidebar, or Access Denied.** Your account can't see
  outcomes. Ask an admin.
- **The outcome isn't on the list.** Check the spelling, and check **Type**
  in case a type is ticked. An animal still in care has no outcome yet.
- **A detail is wrong.** Staff who record outcomes can correct it. Ask them.
