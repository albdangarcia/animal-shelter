## When to use this

Use **Batch entry** when several animals got, or need, the same vaccine or
treatment: a room, a litter, a foster home. It takes up to 50 animals. Tests
are recorded one at a time, since each animal has its own results.

If the care is already on each animal's due list, record it from the Due page
instead, with **Record selected as given**: see
[Schedule care and read the Due page](./schedule-care-and-read-the-due-page.md#record-several-animals-at-once).

## Pick the animals

1. In the sidebar, open **Medical**, then **Batch entry**.
2. Under **Animals**, the shelter's animals are grouped by where they are:
   each **Location** and its units, **In foster with** each foster, and **Not
   housed**. To see fewer, pick one under **Species**.
3. Tick each animal, or tick **Select all** next to a place to pick every
   animal in it.

The box at the top says how many you picked, for example "3 animals picked",
and lists them. Click **Clear** to start again.

## Record care you gave

1. Under **Care**, leave **This care is** on **Given**.
2. Under **Kind**, pick **Vaccine** or **Treatment**.
3. Under **Item**, pick the item. Its usual details fill in, such as a
   vaccine's **Manufacturer** or a treatment's **Route**.
4. Check **Date given**. It starts on today.
5. Under **Given**, pick **In house** or **Outside vet**.
6. For **Outside vet**, pick the clinic under **Vet clinic**, or type it under
   **Vet or clinic name**.
7. Fill in what you know. They are recorded the same for every animal.
   - A vaccine: **Manufacturer**, **Lot number**, **Lot expiry** and **Site**.
   - A treatment: the **Dose** and its unit, and the **Route**.
   - A rabies vaccine also needs the **Duration (months)**, and asks for the
     **Veterinarian name** and **Veterinarian license number**. Under
     **Rabies tag numbers**, type each animal's tag, or leave one empty if it
     has no tag yet.
8. If the item repeats, **Schedule the next one** is already ticked, with a
   **Next due date** worked out from the date given. Check the day, or untick
   the box if no next dose should be planned. An animal that already has the
   item scheduled keeps that one instead.
9. Click **Record for 3 animals** (the number is how many you picked).

You should see "Recorded for 3 animals." With the next dose ticked, it goes
on, for example, "Next dose scheduled for 2. 1 already had one planned." The
form clears for the next batch. Each animal's **Medical** tab shows the entry
under **Recent entries**, and the next dose under **Due**.

## Schedule care for later

1. Under **Care**, pick **Scheduled** under **This care is**.
2. Pick the **Kind** and the **Item**.
3. Pick the **Due date**.
4. Add **Notes** if whoever gives it needs to know something.
5. Click **Schedule for 2 animals** (the number is how many you picked).

You should see "Scheduled for 2 animals." Each animal's **Medical** tab shows
the care under **Due**, and the Due page lists it once it is 7 days away or
less.

## If something goes wrong

- **"FVRCP isn't for Rocket (Dog). Unpick it, or the batch is refused."**
  This shows under **Item** before you save: the item isn't for one of the
  animals you picked. Untick that animal.
- **"Nothing was saved: Rocket was refused."** One or more animals couldn't
  take the care. The box above the button lists each one and why, for example
  "Rocket (Dog): FVRCP isn't for this animal's species." Nothing was saved for
  any animal. Untick those animals, or fix what it says, and click the button
  again.
- **"The due date is required."** Pick a **Due date**.
- **The Record or Schedule button is greyed out.** No animals are picked, or
  more than 50 are ("A batch can cover at most 50 animals."). Untick some and
  record them in a second batch.
- **"Pick a clinic or give the vet's name."** Care given by an outside vet must
  name the vet.
- **No Batch entry in the sidebar.** Batch entry needs a staff member who
  manages medical care. Ask them.
