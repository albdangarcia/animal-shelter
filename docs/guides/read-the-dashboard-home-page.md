## When to use this

The **Dashboard** is the first page you see after you sign in. It shows the
shelter as it is right now, in a few cards, and each card links to the page
behind it. Use it to see what needs doing today and to jump there.

For numbers over a period, such as the live release rate this year, use the
**Reports** instead.

## Steps

1. In the sidebar, click **Dashboard**.
2. Read the four counts across the top: **In care**, **Available**,
   **Pending adoption** and **Space**.
3. Read **Needs attention today**, then the cards beside and below it.
4. Click a card's link to open the page behind it. Click an animal's name to
   open that animal.

The page shows the record as it is when it loads. Reload it to see changes
other people made since.

## What each card shows

| Card | It shows | Its link opens |
|---|---|---|
| **In care** | Every animal on the shelter's books, split into on site, in foster and not housed (in the shelter but in no unit yet). | **Open the shelter board**: the Housing Board. |
| **Available** | Animals published for adoption, and how many drafts aren't published yet. | **List available animals**: the **Animals** list, showing published animals only. |
| **Pending adoption** | Animals with an adoption under way, and how many of them are in a foster-to-adopt placement. | **List pending adoptions**: the **Animals** list, showing those animals only. |
| **Space** | Animals on site against the places in all units, for example "30 / 16", and how many units are full or over capacity. | **See the units**: the Housing Board. |
| **Needs attention today** | Groups of things that are due, overdue or stuck. See below. | Each group's own page. |
| **Under care** | Animals with an open care episode, counted by kind, and the five most recent. | Click an animal to open its medical record. The card has no link of its own. |
| **Applications waiting** | Adoption and foster applications that are **Pending** or **Reviewing**, and how many days the oldest has waited. | The **Adoption Applications** or **Foster Applications** list, showing those applications only, oldest first. |
| **Readiness** | How many animals are ready for adoption, the drafts that are ready but not yet published (click one to open it), how many published animals still have something outstanding, and the biggest blockers. | **Open the readiness board**, or click a blocker or the published line to open the board at that group. With no animals in care, the card has no link. |
| **Longest stays** | Animals in care now, how many have been here over 90 days, and the five longest current stays. | **Length of stay report**. |
| **Intakes vs. Outcomes** | A chart of intakes and outcomes per day. Click **Last 90 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Last 7 days** to change the span. Reversed outcomes aren't counted. | Nothing. |

**Available**, **Pending adoption** and **Applications waiting** count the
same animals or applications as the list their link opens. **In care** and
**Space** open the Housing Board, which shows every unit.

### Needs attention today

Each group appears only when it has something in it. Each item clears when
someone acts on it: records the dose, completes the task, and so on.
Animals that have left the shelter are never listed.

| Group | It counts | The heading opens |
|---|---|---|
| **tasks due or overdue** | Open tasks due today or earlier. | **Animal Tasks**, showing the same open tasks with **Due** set to **Overdue** and **Due today** and **Hide animals that have left** on, the soonest due first. |
| **animal missed a dose** | Animals with a medication dose, yesterday or today, whose time has passed with nothing recorded. A dose recorded as refused or not given doesn't count as missed. | The **Dose sheet**. |
| **care items due or overdue** | Scheduled vaccines, tests, treatments, surgeries, microchips and exams due today or overdue. | The **Due** page, which also lists care due in the next 7 days and exemptions up for review. |
| **animals under care with nothing planned** | Animals with an open care episode and no open task, no scheduled care and no medication running. | Nothing. Click an animal to open its medical record. |
| **foster stay past the return date** | Animals still in foster after the date they were expected back. | Nothing. Click an animal to open it. |

Each group shows its first three animals. Click an animal to open the part of
its record where the item is fixed: its tasks, its medications or its
medical record.

When nothing is due, the card says **"Nothing needs attention today."**

## Which cards you see

Each card needs its own permissions as well as the Dashboard's. A card you
can't read isn't shown at all, and there is no error. Volunteers and staff
see every card.

- **In care**, **Available**, **Pending adoption**, **Space** and the chart
  need only the Dashboard.
- **Needs attention today** also needs animals, tasks and medical records.
- **Under care** also needs medical records.
- **Applications waiting** shows the adoption line if you can read adoption
  applications, and the foster line if you can read fosters.
- **Readiness** also needs animals, assessments, characteristics and medical
  records.
- **Longest stays** also needs the reports.

An account without the Dashboard sees a **Welcome** card instead, with links
to the pages it can open.

## If something goes wrong

- **A card is missing.** Your account can't read what it counts. Ask an
  admin.
- **A count is out of date.** Reload the page.
- **The care items count is smaller than the Due page.** The Due page also
  lists care due in the next 7 days and spay or neuter exemptions up for
  review.
