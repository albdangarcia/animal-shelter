## When to use this

Use this to decide an application someone has sent in. You read it, move it
through the statuses, and tell the applicant why. To find the application
first, see "Find an adoption application" in **Help**. What each status
means is in
[the glossary](./adoption-application-status-glossary.md).

Approving an application does not finish the adoption. An adoption is
finished by recording an outcome (steps below).

## Open the application

1. In the sidebar, click **Adoption Applications**.
2. At the end of the application's row, click the **…** button, then
   **Review**. You can also do this from an animal's **Applications** tab, or
   from a person's **Adoption Applications** tab. The person's tab shows the
   **…** button only to staff who can edit people's records.

The page **Staff Actions** opens: "Reviewing application from" the
applicant "for" the animal. Click the animal's name to open its record, or
**Back to Applications** to go back. Under **Staff Actions** you can read
the applicant's answers under **Applicant Information (Read-Only)**,
**Home & Lifestyle (Read-Only)** and **Experience & Intent (Read-Only)**.

If an answer is wrong, click **Edit Application Fields** (see "Edit an
adoption application for an applicant" in **Help**). That page needs
permission to edit people's records, so without it you get Access Denied. Two
notes can appear above the answers:

- "Entered by staff on behalf of the applicant, so these answers were
  transcribed rather than typed by them." Check it with the applicant if
  something looks wrong.
- **Edited after submission**: who last changed the answers, and when.
  Anything you read before then may be out of date.

If the row has no **…** button, your account can't review applications.

## Change the status

1. Under **Application Status**, pick the next status. The list shows only
   the ones allowed from here (see the glossary).
2. Fill in **Reason for Status Change**. It is required, except when you move
   a **Pending** application to **Reviewing**. The applicant reads it on
   their application page, so write it for them.
3. If you want to leave a note for staff, write it under **Internal
   Notes**. The applicant never sees it. You can save a note without
   changing the status.
4. Click **Update Application**.

You should see "Application updated successfully." and the **Adoption
Applications** list opens (or the person's tab, if you opened the review from
there). Open the application again: **Status History** at the bottom now has a line with the new status, your name, the time and
your reason.

- **Approved** holds the animal. Its card reads **Pending Adoption**, and
  the public page offers no **Adopt** button. While the animal is held, no
  other application can be approved. To approve another, first reject or
  withdraw the approved one.
- **Rejected** and **Withdrawn** can't be changed by staff. The applicant
  can reactivate a withdrawn application, but not a rejected one.
- Changing an **Approved** application to **Rejected** or **Withdrawn**
  releases the animal back to the public listings.
- Use **Withdrawn** only when the applicant has told you they no longer want
  to go ahead. Applicants normally withdraw their own.

## Finish the adoption

An approved application shows **Next Step: Finalize Adoption** with a
**Create Outcome** button, shown only to staff who can record outcomes. Click it to record the adoption: see "Record an
adoption, transfer out, return to owner or other outcome" in **Help**. When
the outcome is saved, this application reads
**Adopted**, and every other open application for the animal reads
**Closed**. Their status can't be changed after that, and an **Adopted**
application can't be edited at all. You can still save an **Internal Note**
on a **Closed** one.

An application that reads **Adopted** or **Closed** has a greyed-out
**Application Status** menu. If the adoption was recorded by mistake, an admin can reverse
the outcome, and the application goes back to **Approved**.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Validation Error: A reason for the status change is required."**
  Fill in **Reason for Status Change**.
- **"This animal is no longer available for adoption. Another application
  may have just been approved."** Approving needs the animal to be
  **Published**. Another application may already be approved, or the animal
  may be **Pending Adoption** for another reason (a foster-to-adopt placement,
  for example) or a **Draft**. Open the animal's record and check its listing
  status. If an application is approved, reject or withdraw it first.
- **"This application is now closed. Reload it and review again."** (it may
  say another status) Someone changed the application, or recorded an outcome
  for the animal, after you opened the page. Reload it and look at the
  **Status History**.
- **"Cannot change a rejected application to approved."** (with the two
  statuses) The change isn't allowed from where the application is now.
  Reload the page.
- **"Adoption Application not found."** The application was deleted. Go back
  to the list.
- **"Access Denied. You do not have permission to perform this action."** Your
  account can't change statuses. Ask a staff member who can.
- **"Database Error: Failed to Update Adoption Application and associated
  records."** Nothing was saved. Try again; if it keeps happening, tell an
  admin.
