## When to use this

Use this each time you weigh an animal, take its temperature or score its
body condition. Volunteers can do this too. The latest weight is the one the
animal's record shows.

Vitals taken during an exam are recorded on the exam by staff who manage
medical care, and then show on the **Vitals** page too. They are corrected
here, like any other entry.

## Record vitals

1. Open the animal, then the **Medical** tab, then **Vitals**. Every entry
   is listed, newest first.
2. Click **Record Vitals**.
3. Under **Weight**, type the weight, and click the unit beside it, for
   example **kg** or **g**.
4. Fill in **Temperature (°C)** or **Body Condition Score (1-9)** if you
   measured them. At least one of the three is needed.
5. Check **Date Recorded**. It starts on today. Days after today can't be
   picked.
6. Add anything else under **Notes**, for example "Weighed after breakfast".
7. Click **Record Vitals**.

You should see "Vitals entry recorded successfully." The entry is at the top
of the list, with the day, "by Sam Rivera" (your name) and what you measured,
for example "Weight: 15.2 kg" and "Body Condition: 5/9". The animal's record
shows the new weight and the change, for example "15.2 kg — up 600 g since
May 20".

## Correct an entry

1. On **Vitals**, click the **…** button on the entry, then **Edit**. The
   box **Edit Vitals Entry** opens.
2. Change what is wrong.
3. Click **Update Vitals**.

You should see "Vitals entry updated successfully."

## Delete and restore an entry

Delete an entry that was recorded by mistake, for example on the wrong
animal.

1. Click the **…** button on the entry, then **Delete**.

You should see "Vitals entry deleted successfully." Click **Undo** on that
message to bring it straight back. The entry leaves the list, and if it held
the latest weight, the animal's record goes back to the weight before it.

A deleted entry isn't gone for good. To see it, click **Status** and tick
**Deleted**: it is listed, marked **Deleted**. Anyone who can record vitals
can restore it:

1. Click the **…** button on the deleted entry, then **Restore**.

You should see "Vitals entry restored successfully."

## If something goes wrong

- **"Record at least one measurement. Notes alone aren't enough."** Fill in
  the weight, the temperature or the body condition score. A note on its own
  belongs on the **Notes** tab.
- **"Temperature must be at least 20°C."** or **"Temperature must be at most
  45°C."** Check the thermometer reading, in degrees Celsius.
- **The browser says the value must be less than or equal to 9 (or greater
  than or equal to 1).** The body condition score is a whole number from 1 to
  9.
- **"Weight must be a positive number."** Check the weight and its unit.
- **"Recorded date cannot be in the future."** Pick today or an earlier day.
- **"Database Error: Failed to record vitals."** Nothing was saved. Try
  again; if it keeps happening, tell an admin.
