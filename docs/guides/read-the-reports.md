## When to use this

The **Reports** count the shelter's intakes and outcomes over a period you
choose: the live release rate, where animals came from, and how long they
stayed. Use them for monthly or yearly numbers.

To see what is happening today, use the **Dashboard** instead. To look up one
intake or outcome, use the **Intakes** or **Outcomes** list.

To take the numbers somewhere else, download a report as a spreadsheet or
print it: see [Download or print a report](#download-or-print-a-report).

For the monthly numbers Shelter Animals Count asks for, use the
**Shelter Animals Count** report: see
[The Shelter Animals Count report](#the-shelter-animals-count-report).

## Pick a period and species

1. In the sidebar, open **Reports**. The period starts as the year to date:
   from January 1 to today. The line under the title shows it, for example
   "Jan 1 – Nov 22, 2026 · Times counted in America/New_York".
2. To choose another period, click **Year to date**. A calendar opens.
3. Click the first day. The report updates to that one day.
4. Click the last day. The report updates to the whole period, and the button
   shows it, for example **Oct 1, 2026 – Oct 31, 2026**.
5. Press Esc or click outside the calendar to close it.
6. To see one kind of animal, click **Species** and tick one or more, for
   example **Cat**. Press Esc to close it. Click **Clear filters** in the same
   list to see every species again.
7. To go back to the year to date, click the period, then **Year to date** at
   the bottom of the calendar. The species you picked stay.

Once a period is set, clicking a day changes one end of it instead of
starting a new period: a day before the start moves the start, a later day
moves the end. To pick a whole new period, go back to the year to date first
(step 7), then open the calendar again and click the first and last days.

Both the first and the last day count in full. Each intake and outcome counts
on the day it was recorded for, so a period is a list of whole days. "Today"
is today in the shelter's time zone, which the page names.

The period and species stay in the page address. They stay when you open a
report from the **Reports** page and when you go back with the **Reports**
link at the top, so you can send a colleague a link to the same numbers.

## The overview

The **Reports** page has four cards:

- **Outcome statistics**: the live release rate, the number of outcomes, and
  the three most common kinds. Click it to open the outcomes report.
- **Intake vs outcome**: how many animals came in and how many left in the
  period, and the difference. It has no report of its own.
- **Length of stay**: the median stay, how many animals have been in care over
  90 days, and how many are in care now. Click it to open the length of stay
  report.
- **Intake sources**: the number of intakes and the two largest sources.
  Click it to open the intake report.

Below them, **Shelter Animals Count** opens the monthly report for Shelter
Animals Count. It counts a month of its own, not the period and species you
picked.

## The outcomes report

**Outcome statistics** shows the **Live release rate**, **Total outcomes**,
**Live outcomes** and **Non-live outcomes**, then each kind of outcome with
its count and its share of all outcomes. In **Outcome detail**, **% of live**
is each live kind's share of the live outcomes.

The live release rate is the live outcomes divided by all outcomes in the
period, to one decimal place:

- Live outcomes are **Adoption**, **Return To Owner**, **Transfer Out**,
  **Return To Field** and **Other Live Outcome**.
- Non-live outcomes are **Euthanized**, **Deceased** and **Lost In Care**.

For example, 86 live outcomes out of 97 is 88.7%. The overview card works it
out the same way, so the two always agree. With no outcomes in the period
both show "—" for the rate: the report adds "No outcomes recorded in this
period." and the card "No outcomes in this period".

## The intake report

**Intake sources** shows:

- **Total intakes**, **Largest source** and **Transfers in**.
- **Monthly intake trend**: intakes in each calendar month of the period.
- **Intakes by source type**: each kind of intake, such as **Stray** or
  **Owner Surrender**, with its count and share, largest first.
- **Top transfer-in partners**: the partners that transferred the most animals
  in, up to ten. It only appears when there is at least one transfer in from
  a partner.

Every intake counts, first intakes and re-intakes alike. An animal that came
in twice in the period counts twice.

## The length of stay report

**Length of stay** has two halves.

The period's half counts completed stays: stays that ended with an outcome
in the period.

- **Median days in care**: the middle length of those stays.
- **Longest completed stay**: the longest of them, in days.
- **Completed-stay length distribution**: how many lasted 0–7, 8–30, 31–90 and
  over 90 days.

The other half is about today, whatever period you picked. The species
filter still applies.

- **Animals in care now**.
- **In care over 90 days**: animals whose current stay is longer than 90 days.
- **Longest current stays**: the 20 animals that have waited longest. Click a
  name to open the animal.

How a stay is counted:

- A stay runs from an intake to the next outcome, in whole days. An animal
  that came in and left on the same day stayed 0 days.
- Time in foster is part of the stay. A foster placement doesn't end it.
- An animal that came back after leaving starts a new stay. **Cumulative
  (days)** adds up all of its stays, and shows "same" when there was only
  one.

## The Shelter Animals Count report

Shelter Animals Count (SAC) is the national database of shelter intakes and
outcomes. Each month it asks for the shelter's numbers, by species and age.
This report counts them and lays them out like SAC's monthly form, row for
row, so you can copy them into SAC.

1. In the sidebar, open **Reports**, then click **Shelter Animals Count**
   below the four cards.
2. The report starts on last month, for example **October 2026**. The line
   under the title shows the month and its days, for example
   "October 2026 · Oct 1 – Oct 31, 2026".
3. To count another month, click the month button beside
   **Download CSV** and pick one. The last 36 whole months are listed. The
   current month isn't, because SAC asks for whole months: it appears once
   it has ended. The month stays in the page address.
4. Read **Check these before you submit** first, if the page shows it. See
   [Warnings](#warnings) below.
5. Copy each table into SAC's form for the same species.

### The tables

There is one table for each SAC species, such as **Canines** and
**Felines**. Each of the shelter's species is counted under the SAC species
an admin picked for it in **Settings**, **Animal taxonomy**. A species with
no SAC species is left out.

Each table has SAC's rows, with the columns **Adult**, **Up to 5 months**,
**Age unknown** and **Total**:

- **Live intakes**. **Stray/At large** counts strays and animal control
  impounds. **Relinquished by owner** counts owner surrenders, returns
  included. **Impound/Seizure** counts seizures. **Other intakes** counts
  animals born in care. **Transferred in from agency** is split into
  **(in state)**, **(out of state)** and **(international)** by the partner
  the animal came from.
- **Live outcomes**: **Adoption**, **Returned to owner**, the three
  **Transferred out to agency** rows (split by the partner the animal went
  to), **Returned to field** and **Other live outcomes**.
- **Other outcomes**: **Died in care** counts the outcome **Deceased**,
  **Lost in care**, and **Shelter euthanasia** counts **Euthanized**.
- **Total outcomes**: the live and other outcomes together.
- **Animal counts**: **Beginning count** is the animals in care, and in
  foster, at the end of the day before the 1st. **Ending count** is the same
  at the end of the month's last day. **Total in care** includes animals in
  foster: an animal counts in foster only while it is also in care.

How the numbers are counted:

- An intake counts in the month it was recorded for, by the animal's age
  that day. An outcome counts by the animal's age on the outcome day. An
  animal is **Up to 5 months** until the day it turns 5 months old.
- **Age unknown** is always 0, because every animal has a birth date.
- Both **Owner intended euthanasia** rows are always 0. The app has no such
  intake: SAC counts owner-requested euthanasia as a community service.
- A **Service In** intake, such as boarding, is not counted anywhere: not
  the intake, not the time in care, and not the outcome that ends it.
- A reversed outcome is not counted. The animal stays in care.
- A transfer partner outside the US is international. A US partner in the
  shelter's state is in state, and one in another state is out of state.
  Washington, DC and the US territories, such as Puerto Rico, count as
  states.

Under each table's name, a line checks the numbers the way SAC does:
beginning count + live intakes − total outcomes should equal the ending
count. "Balanced" means they agree. "Doesn't balance" means a record is
wrong; the warnings name it.

### Warnings

**Check these before you submit** lists what makes the numbers unreliable.
Click an animal's name to open it.

- **"Two intakes in a row with no outcome between them"**, **"Two outcomes
  in a row with no intake between them"** or **"Outcome on … with no intake
  before it"**: the animal's history is missing a record or has one too
  many. Fix the intake or outcome, then reload the report.
- **"Transfer in on … has no partner. Counted as in state."** or
  **"… has no state or country. Counted as in state."**: SAC has no row for
  a transfer it can't place, so it is counted as in state. Add the partner
  to the intake or outcome, or the partner's state or country to the
  partner.
- **"In an open foster placement at the end of … but not in care then"**:
  the animal's foster placement was never ended after its outcome, or the
  animal is in for a service such as boarding. It isn't counted in foster.
  End the placement, or fix the intake or outcome.
- **"The shelter's state isn't set"**: a transfer this month has a US
  partner, and it was counted as in state because the shelter's own state
  isn't set. An admin can set it in **Settings**, **Shelter details**.

A problem in an animal's history shows on the reports for the months it
affects. One that was over before the month started doesn't, because the
records are right again from its last record on.

### Download the SAC report

**Download CSV** saves the month as a file named after it, for example
`shelter-animals-count-2026-10.csv`. It starts with the month, its first and
last day, the shelter's state and the time zone. Then come the tables, in
the page's order and with the same rows, then the warnings.

The file is laid out like SAC's form, not like SAC's upload template. SAC
only offers its template from inside a SAC account, so the app can't match
its column names. Copy the numbers into SAC rather than uploading the file.

## Download or print a report

Each report page, the **Reports** page included, has a **Download CSV**
button beside **Species**. The Shelter Animals Count report has one beside
its month: see [Download the SAC report](#download-the-sac-report).

1. Pick the period and species first. The file counts what the page shows.
2. Click **Download CSV**. The browser saves a file named after the report
   and the period, for example
   `outcome-statistics-2026-01-01-to-2026-11-22.csv`.
3. Open it in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers.

The file starts with the report's name, the first and last day, the species
and the time zone. Then come the page's tables, one after another, with the
same numbers and percentages as the screen: "88.7%", and "—" where the page
shows "—". The monthly intake trend has a column for the year and one for
the month. The length of stay file lists the 20 longest current stays with
their intake dates.

To print a report, or to save it as a PDF, use the browser's **Print**
(Cmd+P on a Mac, Ctrl+P on Windows) and pick **Save as PDF** as the printer
if you want a file. The printout leaves out the sidebar, the buttons and the
filters, and says which species it counts under the title. It prints in
light colours even when the app is in dark mode.

## When a record is corrected

The reports read the records as they are now:

- A corrected intake or outcome counts with its corrected date.
- A reversed outcome counts for nothing. The animal's stay carries on as if
  it never left, and the outcome no longer counts in the live release rate.
- A re-intake is a new intake and a new stay, even when it was recorded to
  undo a mistake. That is why a mistaken outcome is reversed, not undone with
  a re-intake.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Access Denied", or no Reports in the sidebar.** Your account can't read
  the reports. Ask an admin.
- **A number looks too small.** Check the period and **Species**. The period
  starts as the year to date, which may not cover what you want.
- **The button shows "From …" or "Until …".** The link you opened set only a
  start or only an end. The other end is today, or January 1 of this year.
- **A period longer than two years** is cut to the last two years of it.
- **A number doesn't match what you remember.** Look the records up on the
  **Intakes** or **Outcomes** list. A reversed outcome is on the list, marked
  **Reversed**, but the reports don't count it.
- **The downloaded file shows odd characters instead of "—".** The
  spreadsheet opened it with the wrong text encoding. In Excel, use
  **Data**, then **From Text/CSV**, and pick **UTF-8**.
- **A name in the file starts with an apostrophe.** A name that starts with
  `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` gets one, so the spreadsheet shows it as text instead
  of running it as a formula.
