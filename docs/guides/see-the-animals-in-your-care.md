## When to use this

Use this to see which animals the shelter has placed with you, and for how
long. To give medication, record a treatment or report a vet visit, see
"Fosters: give care and report an outside vet visit" in **Help**. If you
haven't applied to foster yet, see "Apply to foster, check your application and
withdraw it" in **Help**.

## See your animals

1. In the sidebar, click **My Foster Animals**.

The page **My foster animals** lists the animals currently placed with you.
Each card shows:

- the animal's name and a badge with the kind of placement: **General**,
  **Medical**, **Foster To Adopt** or **Long Term**. The shelter picks it.
- its species, breed and age.
- "Since" and the day it came to you. If the shelter set a day it should come
  back, "Expected return" and that day. It is the shelter's estimate. After
  the day, the card adds "(date passed)". Contact the shelter if plans have
  changed.
- **Medical care**, which opens the animal's care page.

The name is a link to the public page for the animal, when it is listed
there.

## See your past fosters

Under **Past fosters** are your five most recent placements that ended, with
the animal, the kind of placement and the days it started and ended. An animal
appears here once the shelter has brought it back, or has recorded that it left.

## If something goes wrong

- **"You're not on the foster roster yet. Submit a foster application to get
  started."** The shelter hasn't approved you yet. Click **Apply to Foster**, or
  open **My Foster Application** to see where your application stands.
- **"You're not currently fostering any animals."** The shelter hasn't placed an
  animal with you, or the animals have gone back. Contact the shelter if you
  expected one.
- **An animal you have is missing.** Contact the shelter. Only the shelter can
  place or return animals.
