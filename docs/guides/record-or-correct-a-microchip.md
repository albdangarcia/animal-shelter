## When to use this

Use this when an animal has been chipped here or by a vet, when a scan finds a
chip it already had, or when its registration changes (sent to a registry,
then registered). The chip shows on the animal's profile and on its
**Medical** tab, with its registration.

## Record a chip

1. Open the animal, then the **Medical** tab.
2. If a microchip is under **Due**, click **Record** on it. Otherwise click
   **Add entry**, then **Microchip**.
3. Type the **Chip number** as it reads on the scanner or certificate. Spaces
   and hyphens are fine: they are taken out when it is saved.
4. Fill in the **Brand** if you know it.
5. Under **Given**, pick **In house**, **Outside vet** or **Before intake**
   (a chip found on a scan at intake). **Before intake** is only offered from
   **Add entry**, not when you record a chip from **Due**.
6. Check **Date given**. For a chip from before intake, tick **Date unknown**
   if nobody knows the day.
7. Under **Chip registration**, set **Registration status**: **Unknown**,
   **Not registered**, **Pending** or **Registered**. What each means: see
   the [glossary](./medical-status-glossary.md#microchip-registration).
   - **Pending** or **Registered**: type the **Registry name**.
   - **Registered**: add the **Registration date** if you know it.
8. Click **Record Microchip**.

You should see "Entry recorded." On **Overview**, **Microchip** shows the
number and "Registration: Registered · *registry* · Date unknown" (or the
day).

## Correct the chip or its registration

1. Open the animal, then the **Medical** tab, then **History**.
2. Find the microchip and click its **…** menu, then **Edit**.
3. Change what is wrong, for example set **Registration status** to
   **Registered** and type the **Registry name**.
4. Click **Save Changes**.

You should see "Entry updated." Under the entry, **Show 1 change** lists what
changed.

If you change the **Chip number**, the registration is cleared: the form says
"The chip number changed. Enter registration details for this number, or
leave Unknown when they aren't known." Fill them in again before you save, or
the chip is saved with **Unknown** registration.

A chip recorded on the wrong animal is voided, not edited: see the guide on
correcting or voiding an entry.

## If something goes wrong

- **"The chip number is required."** Type the number.
- **"A chip number is 9 to 15 letters or digits."** Check the number. Spaces
  and hyphens don't count.
- **"A chip number has only letters and digits."** Take out any other
  characters.
- **"That chip number is already on Rocky's record."** Another animal has this
  chip. Check the number. If the other record is wrong, void the chip there
  first.
- **"That chip number is already on this animal's record."** The chip is
  recorded already. Edit that entry instead.
- **"A registered chip needs its registry name."** Type the **Registry name**.
- **No Add entry or no … menu.** Recording a chip nobody scheduled, and
  correcting one, need a staff member who manages medical care. Ask them.
