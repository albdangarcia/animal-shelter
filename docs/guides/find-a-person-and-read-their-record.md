## When to use this

Use this to find someone the shelter has on record, such as an adopter, a
surrenderer, a finder, a volunteer or a foster, and to read what the shelter
holds about them. A read-only account can do everything here. To add or
change a record you need an account that can edit people, so ask a staff
member.

Administrator logins don't appear in the People Directory. Staff, volunteer
and applicant logins do. There is no way to delete or merge a person, so check
before you add someone.

## Find a person

1. In the sidebar, click **People Directory**. The newest records are at the
   top. Each row shows **Name**, **Account**, **Email**, **Phone**, **City**
   and **State**. **Account** reads **Registered** when the person has a login
   and **No Account** when they are only a contact record.
2. Type in **Filter by name, email, or phone...**. The list shows people whose
   name, email or phone contains what you typed.
3. To search by phone, type the digits however you like, for example
   `2125550188` or `(212) 555-0188`. It finds the person however their number
   was written, as long as you type at least four digits. A number the app
   couldn't read as a phone number, such as "call the front desk", is found
   only when you type it as it was written. The app reads ordinary phone
   numbers, with or without brackets, dashes, dots or a +1.
4. To see only people with a login, or only people without one, click
   **Account** and tick **Registered** or **No Account**. A **Reset** button
   appears once you've filtered. Click it to see everyone again.
5. To sort, click a column's name, then **Asc** or **Desc**. **Customize
   Columns** hides columns, and **Rows per page** shows more.
6. Click the person's name to open their record.

Several people can share a name, so check the email and phone before you pick
one. If nothing matches, the list says "No results." Try less of the name.

## Read a record

The top of the record shows the name, **Registered User** or **Contact
Record**, the address and a badge with their role or **No Account**. A person
whose login has been turned off also shows **Deactivated**. Four tiles count
**Applications**, **Animal Events** (animals they surrendered, found or
reclaimed), **Role** and **Email Verified**.

The tabs under it:

- **Profile**: **Contact & Account Details** (the address, then **Login
  Account**, and for a person with a login the **Sign-in Email**, **Role** and
  **Email Verified**) and **Household & Lifestyle**.
- **Animal History**: every animal the person is linked to, tagged
  **Surrenderer**, **Finder**, **Owner Reclaimed**, **Applicant** (with the
  application's status) or **Foster Carer**. The first ten show. A button
  such as **Show 5 More** shows the rest.
- **Adoption Applications**: see "Find an adoption application" in **Help**.
- **Fostering**: shown only if your account can read fosters. See "Find a
  foster or a placement" in **Help**.
- **Staff Activity**: what a shelter worker did on animals: intakes and
  outcomes they processed, tasks they created or were given, and animal notes
  they wrote. **Show details** opens the intake or outcome type, or the note's
  text. For most people it says "No Activity Found".
- **Notes**: what staff wrote about this person, newest first. Staff can add
  and edit them. You can read them.

## If something goes wrong

- **Access Denied**. Your account can't read the People Directory. Ask an
  admin.
- **Person Not Found**. The link is wrong, or it is an administrator's record,
  which the directory doesn't show. Go back to **People Directory**.
- **Add Person** is greyed out, or the rows have no **…** button. Your account
  can read people but not change them. Ask a staff member.
- A person you expected isn't listed. Search by email or phone instead of
  name. If they still aren't there, ask a staff member to add them.
