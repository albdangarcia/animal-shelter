## When to use this

Use this when an adopter, a new owner or another shelter needs the animal's
medical record on paper, or proof of its rabies vaccination. Each printout
shows the record as it is when you open it: a copy you printed earlier
doesn't change when the record does.

## Print the adopter medical history

1. Open the animal, then the **Medical** tab. **Overview** opens.
2. Click **Print adopter medical history** (it is also on **History**).
3. The page **Medical history —** *name* opens. It shows the animal's
   details, a summary (spay/neuter, rabies, microchips), then completed care,
   conditions and allergies, care episodes, medication orders, care still
   planned, and the names of its documents.
4. Click **Print / Save as PDF**. Your browser's print window opens: print
   it, or pick **Save as PDF** there.
5. Click **Return to medical record** to go back.

The handout leaves out voided entries, who changed what, and end-of-life
records.

## Print a rabies certificate

1. Open the animal, then the **Medical** tab, then **Vaccines & Tests** (or
   **History**).
2. Find the rabies vaccine and click **Print rabies certificate** under it.
3. The page **Rabies vaccination certificate —** *name* opens. It shows the
   animal, its microchips, the vaccine, lot, tag, the day given, how long it
   is valid, and the veterinarian's name and license number. It says whether
   the vaccination is still valid, for example "Recorded valid through Nov 8,
   2027 (inclusive)."
4. Click **Print / Save as PDF**, then sign on the **Veterinarian signature**
   line once printed.
5. Click **Return to medical record** to go back.

## If something goes wrong

Instead of **Print rabies certificate**, the vaccine may say "Certificate
unavailable" and why:

- **"Record the veterinarian's name and license number before printing a
  certificate."** The vaccine was recorded without them. Staff who manage
  medical care can add **Veterinarian name** and **Veterinarian license
  number** by editing the entry.
- **"Record the vaccination date before printing a certificate."** The day
  it was given isn't known, for example a vaccine from before intake. Staff
  who manage medical care can add it by editing the entry, if the paperwork
  shows it.
- **No Print rabies certificate, and no "Certificate unavailable", on a
  vaccine.** Only a rabies vaccine that was given, with its duration, and
  isn't voided has a certificate.
- **The printout shows something wrong.** Fix the entry, then open the
  printout again. Staff who manage medical care correct entries on
  **History**.
