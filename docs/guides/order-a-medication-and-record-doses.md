## When to use this

Use this when an animal needs a medication on a schedule, such as an
antibiotic twice a day, or as needed, such as pain relief.

- An **order** says what to give, how much, how and how often, and from when
  to when. Staff who manage medical care order it.
- Each **dose** is recorded when it is given, refused or not given.
- The **Dose sheet** lists every dose due across the shelter, so one person
  can work through a round.

What **Due** and **Missed** mean on a dose: see the
[glossary](./medical-status-glossary.md#medication-doses).

## Order a medication

1. Open the animal, then the **Medical** tab, then **Medications**.
2. Click **Order medication**.
3. Under **Medication**, pick it. Its usual **Dose**, **Unit** and **Route**
   fill in.
4. Check the **Dose**, **Unit** and **Route**.
5. Under **How often**, pick **Once a day**, **Twice a day**, **Three times a
   day**, **Every few days** or **As needed**. For **Every few days**, type
   the days under **Every how many days**.
6. Check the **Start date**, and the **First dose** if it shows. They start
   on the next dose time that hasn't ended yet, which late in the day is
   tomorrow's first one.
7. To end the course on a set day, pick the **End date**, or type the number
   of days under **For how many days** and the end date fills in. Left empty,
   it is given until it is discontinued.
8. Fill in **Prescribed by** and **Instructions** (for example "With food.").
9. Click **Order Medication**.

You should see "Medication ordered." The order appears under **Current
orders**. If it starts today it reads **Active**, with today's dose times
under it. If it starts on a later day it reads **Starts** and the day, with
"First dose:" and the time. See the
[glossary](./medical-status-glossary.md#medication-orders).

The medication, dose and how often can't change once ordered. To change them,
discontinue the order and order again.

## Record a dose on the animal's page

1. On **Medications**, find the order. Under **Today**, each dose time
   (**AM**, **Midday**, **PM**) shows **Due**, **Missed**, or what was
   recorded.
2. When you have given it, click **Give** on that time.

You should see "Dose recorded as given." The time reads, for example,
"Given 8:15 AM · Olivia Chen".

If the dose wasn't given as planned, click the arrow next to **Give**:

- **Refused…**: the animal wouldn't take it.
- **Not given…**: it wasn't given. Say why under **Why not**.
- **Record with details…**: it was given at another time, or you want to add
  notes.

Fill in the box, then click **Record Dose**. You should see, for example,
"Dose recorded as not given." A refused or not given dose is accounted for,
so it doesn't turn **Missed**.

Earlier days are under **Dose sheet** on the same page: the 7 days before
today. A **Missed** dose there can still be recorded with **Give** if it was
given late.

## Work through the Dose sheet

1. In the sidebar, open **Medical**, then **Dose sheet**.
2. The page shows today. Each dose time (**AM**, **Midday**, **PM**) says when
   it ends, for example "ends at 10:00 AM". Under it, doses are grouped by
   where the animal is: its **Location**, **In foster with** a foster, or
   **Not housed**. Orders given as needed are under **As needed**.
3. To see an earlier day, click the previous day's date, with the left
   arrow, at the top. You can go back 7 days.
4. Click **Give** on a dose you gave, or use the arrow next to it, as on the
   animal's page.

### Record several doses at once

1. Tick the box on each dose you gave.
2. Click **Record selected as given** (it shows how many you picked).
3. The box lists each dose. They are all recorded as given now, by you.
4. Click **Record 2 as given** (the number is how many you picked).

You should see "Recorded 2 doses as given." If any of them can't be
recorded, none is.

## Stop or fix an order

On the order, click its **…** menu:

- **Discontinue…**: the medication stops. Pick the day under **Discontinue
  from** (no dose is expected from that day on), type the **Reason**, and click
  **Discontinue**. You should see "Order discontinued." The order moves to
  **Past orders** with "Why it stopped:" and your reason.
- **Edit**: correct the **End date**, **Prescribed by** or **Instructions**.
  The start can only change while no dose is recorded. Click **Save
  Changes**.
- **Order again**: start a new order with the same medication, dose, how
  often, instructions and prescriber filled in, starting from the next dose
  time and running for the same number of days. If the medication has been
  retired since, pick it again.
- **Void…**: the order should never have been entered. Its doses stop
  counting.

To void one dose recorded by mistake (wrong time, wrong day, wrong animal),
click the void button (a circle with a line through it) next to it, type
why, then click **Void Dose**. The button stays greyed out until you type a
reason. It stays on the sheet, struck through, and the time can be
recorded again.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Pick a medication."**, **"Give the dose."**, **"Pick the dose's
  unit."**, **"Pick the route."** or **"Pick how often."** Fill in every field
  marked with *.
- **"The dose must be more than 0."** Check the **Dose**.
- **"Give the days between doses."** An order given every few days needs the
  days between doses.
- **"The end date can't be before the start date."** Check both days.
- **"Say why the dose wasn't given."** Fill in **Why not**.
- **"Say why the order is being discontinued."** Fill in **Reason**.
- **No Order medication button, or no … menu on an order.** Ordering and
  changing medication need a staff member who manages medical care. Ask them.
- **No Give button.** Your account can only read medical records, or the
  animal has left the shelter's care. Ask an admin if you should be able to
  record doses.
- **No void button next to a dose.** Voiding a dose needs a staff member who
  manages medical care. Ask them.
- **"Database Error: Failed to record the dose."** Nothing was saved. Try
  again; if it keeps happening, tell an admin.
