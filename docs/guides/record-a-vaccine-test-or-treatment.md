## When to use this

Use this after an animal has been given a vaccine, test or treatment, by the
shelter or by an outside vet. If the care was scheduled, record that entry so
it leaves the due list. If it wasn't scheduled, add a new entry.

To plan care for later, see
[Schedule care and read the Due page](./schedule-care-and-read-the-due-page.md).
An entry recorded with a mistake is corrected or voided by staff who manage
medical care.

## Record care that was scheduled

1. Open the animal, then the **Medical** tab. **Overview** opens.
2. Under **Due**, find the care you gave and click **Record**.
   (You can also click **Record** on the same row of the
   [Due page](./schedule-care-and-read-the-due-page.md).)
3. A form opens, titled with the item, for example **Record DHPP as given**.
   The item's usual details are already filled in.
4. Under **Given**, pick **In house** or **Outside vet**. For **Outside vet**,
   you must name the vet: pick the clinic under **Vet clinic**, or type the
   vet or clinic under **Vet or clinic name**.
5. Check **Date given**. It starts on today.
6. Fill in what you know, such as **Manufacturer**, **Lot number** and
   **Site** for a vaccine. These are optional.
7. If the item repeats, **Schedule the next one** is ticked and **Next due
   date** is filled in. Change the date, or untick the box if the animal
   doesn't need another.
8. Click **Record Vaccine** (or **Record Test**, **Record Treatment**).

You should see "Recorded. Next DHPP due Nov 23, 2026." (or "Entry recorded."
when nothing repeats). The entry moves from **Due** to **Recent entries**, and
the next dose appears under **Due**.

## Record care that wasn't scheduled

1. Open the animal, then the **Medical** tab.
2. Click **Add entry** and pick **Vaccine**, **Test** or **Treatment**.
3. Pick the item first, in the field named after the kind (for example
   **Test**). Its usual details fill in the rest.
4. Under **Given**, pick **In house**, **Outside vet** or **Before intake**
   (care the animal had before it came to the shelter).
5. For **Outside vet**, you must name the vet: pick the clinic under **Vet
   clinic**, or pick **Not a partner clinic** and type it under **Vet or
   clinic name**. Care in house or before intake needs no vet.
6. Check **Date given**.
7. For a test, choose a result for each line under **Results**.
8. Fill in anything else you know, then click **Record Test** (or
   **Record Vaccine**, **Record Treatment**).

You should see "Entry recorded." The entry appears under **Recent entries**
and on the **Vaccines & Tests** or **History** page.

## If something goes wrong

- **No Add entry button.** Recording care nobody scheduled needs a staff
  member who plans care. Ask them to add it, or to schedule it so you can
  record it.
- **"Pick an item."** Pick the vaccine, test or treatment first.
- **"Choose a result."** A test needs a result for every line under
  **Results**.
- **"The date given can't be in the future."** Pick today or an earlier day.
- **"The date given can't be before the animal's intake on …"** Care from
  before the animal arrived is recorded with **Given** set to
  **Before intake**.
- **"The date given is required. Only care given before intake can have an
  unknown date."** Pick a date, or choose **Before intake** if nobody knows
  when it was given.
- **"Pick a clinic or give the vet's name."** Care given by an outside vet
  must name the vet. Pick the clinic under **Vet clinic**, or type the vet or
  clinic under **Vet or clinic name**.
- **"Pick a vet clinic from the partner directory."** The clinic you picked
  isn't an active vet clinic any more. Pick another, or type the name under
  **Vet or clinic name**.
- **"Access Denied. You do not have permission to perform this action."** Your
  account can't record medical care. Ask an admin.
- **"Database Error: Failed to record entry."** Nothing was saved. Try again;
  if it keeps happening, tell an admin.
