## When to use this

Use this after an animal has been examined, here or by an outside vet. An
intake exam is usually already scheduled by the animal's protocol. The
animal's **Medical** tab shows its last exam under **Last exam**.

## Record a scheduled exam

1. Open the animal, then the **Medical** tab.
2. Under **Due**, find the exam, for example **Intake exam**, and click
   **Record**.
3. The form **Record Intake exam as given** opens. **Exam type** is filled
   in. Go on from step 4 below.

## Record an exam that wasn't scheduled

1. Open the animal, then the **Medical** tab.
2. Click **Add entry**, then **Exam**. The form **Record an exam** opens.
3. Under **Exam type**, pick **Intake exam**, **Vet exam**, **Recheck exam**
   or **Pre-adoption exam**.
4. Under **Given**, pick **In house** or **Outside vet**. For **Outside vet**,
   name the vet.
5. Check **Date given**.
6. Under **Overall result**, pick **No concerns**, **Needs treatment** or
   **Needs vet review**.
7. Under **Findings**, every body system starts on **Not examined**. For each
   system you looked at, pick **Normal** or **Abnormal**.
8. For each **Abnormal** one, a note box opens. Say what was found, for
   example "Mild discharge, left eye". To add a note on another system, click
   **Add note** under it.
9. Under **Reproductive status**, pick **Intact**, **Altered** or
   **Undetermined** if you checked it.
10. Under **Vitals**, fill in the **Weight** (pick the unit),
    **Temperature (°C)** or **Body Condition Score (1-9)** if you measured
    them. They are optional.
11. Click **Record Exam**.

You should see "Entry recorded." On **Overview**, **Last exam** reads, for
example, "Recheck exam, Nov 10, 2026: needs vet review". On **History**, the
exam lists each abnormal finding with its note, then the normal and not
examined systems. Any vitals also appear on the **Vitals** page.

## When the exam finds the animal already altered

1. Under **Reproductive status**, pick **Altered**.
2. **Record the spay or neuter found on this exam** appears, ticked. Under
   **Procedure**, check the spay or neuter. Untick the box if you don't want
   it recorded.
3. Click **Record Exam**.

The spay or neuter is recorded as done before intake, on an unknown date,
with this exam as its evidence. The animal's spay/neuter status becomes
**Altered**. See the [glossary](./medical-status-glossary.md#altered). If a
spay or neuter is on the record already, the box doesn't appear.

## After the exam

- To put a problem the exam found on the animal's problem list, click **Add
  a condition found on this exam** under the exam on **History**. **Found
  on** is filled in with this exam. The button shows only to staff who manage
  medical care, while the animal is in the shelter's care. See the guide on
  care episodes, conditions and allergies.
- Vitals recorded with the exam are corrected on the **Vitals** page, not on
  the exam.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Pick the exam type."** Pick one under **Exam type**.
- **"Pick the overall result."** Pick one under **Overall result**.
- **"Say what was found."** A system marked **Abnormal** needs a note.
- **"Pick spay or neuter."** Pick the **Procedure** under **Record the spay
  or neuter found on this exam**, or untick the box.
- **"Pick a clinic or give the vet's name."** An exam by an outside vet must
  name the vet.
- **"You don't have permission to record vitals."** Your account can't record
  vitals. Clear the **Vitals** fields and record the exam, then ask someone
  who can record vitals to add them on the **Vitals** page.
- **"You don't have permission to record a spay or neuter."** Recording the
  spay or neuter found on the exam needs a staff member who manages medical
  care. Untick **Record the spay or neuter found on this exam**, record the
  exam, and ask them to record it.
- **No Add entry button.** Recording an exam nobody scheduled needs a staff
  member who manages medical care. Ask them, or ask them to schedule it so
  you can record it.
