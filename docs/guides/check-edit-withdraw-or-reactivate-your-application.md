## When to use this

Use this after you've applied to adopt an animal. You can see what the
shelter has decided and why, fix a mistake while your application is still
waiting, withdraw it if you've changed your mind, or bring it back if you
withdrew by accident. To apply for an animal, see
[Apply to adopt an animal](./apply-to-adopt-an-animal.md).

## See where your application stands

1. In the sidebar, click **My Adoption Applications**. Each row shows the
   animal, its **Status** and when you applied. Type an animal's name in
   **Filter by animal name...**, or click **Status** to see one kind.
2. Click the **…** button at the end of a row, then **View application**.

The page shows the animal, a status and a short message that says what it
means:

- **Pending**: "Waiting for review". The shelter hasn't started yet. You can
  still change it.
- **Reviewing**: "Under review". A staff member is reading it, so you can no
  longer change it. If something has changed, contact the shelter and they can
  update it for you.
- **Waitlisted**: "On the waitlist". The shelter has read it and is holding
  it. They will be in touch if the animal becomes available to you.
- **Approved**: "Approved". The animal is being held for you. The shelter will
  contact you to arrange the adoption.
- **Rejected**: "Not moving forward". The shelter decided not to go ahead.
  Any reason is in the history. You can't apply for this animal again, but you
  can apply for others.
- **Withdrawn**: "Withdrawn by you". See below to bring it back.
- **Adopted**: "Adoption complete". The adoption is finished.
- **Closed**: "No longer available". The animal left the shelter while your
  application was still open, even if it was **Approved**: it was adopted by
  someone else, transferred or otherwise left. It is
  not a decision about you, and if the animal is listed again you can apply.

At the bottom, **Status History** lists every change, newest first: the
status, who made it, when, and the reason the shelter gave. This is the place
the shelter explains its decisions, so look there for a reason.

If the shelter fixed something in your answers, **Changed by the shelter**
shows who did it and when. Contact the shelter if it isn't what you meant.

## Change your application

You can change it only while it is **Pending**.

1. Open your application, then click **Edit application**.
2. Change your answers.
3. Click **Update Application**.

You should see "Application updated successfully." and you are taken back to
your list.

## Withdraw your application

You can withdraw while it is **Pending**, **Reviewing**, **Waitlisted** or
**Approved**.

1. Open your application, then click **Withdraw application**.
2. Read the message, then click **Withdraw**. Click **Cancel** to keep it.

You should see "Application withdrawn successfully." and the status reads
**Withdrawn**. If your application was **Approved**, the animal is released and
other people can apply for it.

## Bring a withdrawn application back

1. Open the withdrawn application and click **Reactivate**.

You should see "Application reactivated successfully." It is **Pending**
again, as if you had just applied, and the shelter reviews it from the
start. You can reactivate it only while the animal is open for applications. If
the animal is being held for someone else's adoption, or has left, you can't.

## If something goes wrong

- **No Edit application button.** Your application is no longer **Pending**,
  or has been decided. Contact the shelter if something has changed.
- **No Withdraw application button.** It is already **Withdrawn**, **Rejected**,
  **Adopted** or **Closed**.
- **Reactivate is greyed out, with "Duke has left the shelter, so this
  application can no longer be reactivated."** (with the animal's name) The
  animal isn't open for applications any more: it may have been adopted, or
  be held for another applicant. You are welcome to apply for another.
- **"Cannot reactivate application. This animal is no longer available for
  adoption."** The same, found when you clicked.
- **"Cannot reactivate application. You already have an active application for
  this animal."** Another application of yours for this animal is still open.
- **"Cannot reactivate application. A previous application for this animal was
  not approved."** You can't use reactivation to get past a rejection.
- **"Cannot update application. Its status is currently "Reviewing". Only
  pending applications can be modified."** (with the status) The shelter
  picked your application up while you were editing it. Contact the shelter.
- **"Cannot withdraw application. Its status is currently "Rejected"."** (with
  the status) It is already settled.
- **"Adoption Application not found."** The application isn't yours, or no
  longer exists. Go back to your list.
- **"Database Error: Failed to withdraw Adoption Application."** (or "…reactivate…",
  or "…Update…") Nothing was changed. Try again. If it keeps happening, contact
  the shelter.
