## When to use this

Medication doses are planned for three times of day: **AM**, **Midday** and
**PM**. Each one ends at a time you set. A dose with nothing recorded by then
reads **Missed**. Use this guide when the shelter's round times change.

Ordering medication and recording doses is a different task. Staff do it on
the animal's page and on the Dose sheet.

## Steps

1. In the sidebar, open **Settings**, then the **Medical** card, then the
   **Dose times** tab. It shows when each time ends, for example "AM doses end
   at 10:00 AM".
2. Click **Edit dose times**.
3. Set **AM doses end at**, **Midday doses end at** and **PM doses end at**.
   The times are on the shelter's clock, and AM must come before Midday and
   Midday before PM.
4. Click **Save Dose Times**.

You should see "Dose times saved." The box closes and the page shows the new
times, for example "9:00 AM".

To check, open **Medical**, then **Dose sheet**. Each time says when it ends,
for example "ends at 9:00 AM".

If you close the box with changes not saved, it asks "Discard unsaved dose
times?". Click **Cancel** to go back, or **Discard** to lose the changes.

## What a change does

The new times apply from then on. Doses already recorded keep their slot and
don't move.

A change only affects today. A dose not yet recorded today can turn from
**Due** to **Missed** if you move its end earlier, or back to **Due** if you
move it later. Past days don't change, because every time on a past day has
already ended. The "ends at" heading on a past day on the Dose sheet shows the
new time.

The times also decide where a new medication order starts by default: today at
the first slot that hasn't ended, or tomorrow's first slot when all of today's
have.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Midday doses must end after AM doses."** Set Midday later than AM.
- **"PM doses must end after Midday doses."** Set PM later than Midday.
- **"Enter a time of day, such as 10:00."** One of the boxes is empty or isn't
  a time. Fill in all three.
