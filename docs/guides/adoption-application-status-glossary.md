## When to use this

Use this when an application shows a status and you want to know what it
means, who set it and what can happen next. To change a status, see
"Review an application and change its status" in **Help**.

Six of the statuses are decisions: a person chose each one. **Adopted** and
**Closed** are not. Nobody picks them. They follow from an outcome recorded
for the animal, so you will never find them in the **Application Status**
list.

## The six decisions

- **Pending.** Submitted and waiting. The applicant set it by applying (or
  you did, by entering the application for them). An applicant who
  reactivates a withdrawn application puts it back here.
- **Reviewing.** A staff member has picked it up. It needs no reason.
- **Waitlisted.** Staff reviewed it and are holding it. The word is used two
  ways: a strong application kept as a **backup** for one animal, or an
  applicant who is **approved in general** and waiting for a suitable animal.
  The status doesn't say which. Say which in the reason, because the
  applicant sees it. The applicant's own page says "Another applicant is being
  considered first", which fits the first meaning best.
- **Approved.** Staff said yes. The animal is held: its card reads **Pending
  Adoption** and the public **Adopt** button goes away. While the animal is held,
  no other application can be approved. Approving is not the adoption. The
  adoption is finished by recording an outcome.
- **Rejected.** Staff said no. Staff can't undo it, and the applicant can't
  apply for the same animal again.
- **Withdrawn.** The applicant no longer wants it. Staff can record it too,
  for example after the applicant phones. It can't be undone by staff, but
  the applicant can **Reactivate** it while the animal is open for
  applications.

## The two that follow an outcome

- **Adopted.** An adoption outcome was recorded against this application.
- **Closed.** The animal left the shelter (an adoption by someone else, a
  transfer, a return to owner, a death) while this application was still
  Pending, Reviewing, Waitlisted or Approved. It is not a judgment about the
  applicant, who is free to apply again if the animal is listed again.
  Rejected and Withdrawn applications stay as they were.

Voiding the outcome undoes both: the adopter's application reads **Approved**
again, and each **Closed** application reads as it did before.

## Where to see who changed what

Open the application with **Review**. At the bottom, **Status History** lists
every change, newest first: the status, who made it, when, and the reason.
The applicant sees the same list on their own page, so write each reason for
them to read. An entry for an outcome shows a reason the app writes itself,
for example "Animal adopted by applicant." or "This animal was adopted by
another applicant."

**Internal Notes** are for staff only. The applicant never sees them.

## If something goes wrong

- **The status you want isn't in the menu.** Staff can only move an
  application forward: **Rejected** and **Withdrawn** have nothing after
  them, and **Approved** can only go to **Withdrawn** or **Rejected**. An
  **Adopted** or **Closed** application has the menu greyed out.
