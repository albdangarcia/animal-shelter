## When to use this

Use this to decide an application someone sent in to become a foster. You read
it, move it through the statuses, and tell the applicant why. To find the
application first, see "Find a foster or a placement" in **Help**. This is not
the adoption application: those are under **Adoption Applications**.

Three different things are involved, and approving only does the first two:

- **The application** is the request to become a foster.
- **The profile** is the approved foster on the roster. Approving the
  application creates it.
- **A placement** is an animal actually staying with the foster. Nothing is
  placed by approving. Place an animal as in
  [Place an animal in foster](./place-an-animal-in-foster.md).

To put someone on the roster without an application, see
[Add a foster without an application](./add-a-foster-without-an-application.md).

## Open the application

1. In the sidebar, click **Foster Applications**.
2. Find the row. You can type a name under **Filter by applicant name...**, or
   narrow the list with **Status**.
3. At the end of the row, click the **…** button, then **Review**.

The page opens with the applicant's name and status, and "Foster application
submitted" with the day. Under it are **Applicant Information**, **Household &
Lifestyle**, **Foster Capabilities** (the species, how many animals, and what
they said they can do) and **Status History**. Click **Back to Foster
Applications** to go back.

If the row says **View** and there is no **Application Status** on the page,
your account can only read applications.

## Change the status

1. Under **Application Status**, pick the next status. The list shows only
   the ones allowed from here.
2. Fill in **Reason for Status Change**. It is required for every change,
   including **Reviewing**. The applicant reads it on their application page, so
   write it for them.
3. Click **Update Status**.

You should see "Foster application updated successfully." The page stays
open. **Status History** at the bottom has a new line with the status, your
name, the time and your reason.

- **Reviewing** and **Waitlisted** change nothing else. Waitlisted is for an
  applicant you want to keep for later.
- **Approved** puts the person on the roster (see below).
- **Rejected** and **Withdrawn** can't be changed by staff, and the menu is
  greyed out. The applicant can send a new application.
- **Approved** can only move to **Rejected** or **Withdrawn**. Doing so does
  **not** take the person off the roster. To stop placing animals with them,
  see [Pause, deactivate or change a foster](./pause-deactivate-or-change-a-foster.md).

## What approving creates

The person appears on **Fosters** as **Active**, with the capabilities from
their application: species, **Max Animals**, the yes and no answers and the
availability notes. Their **Fostering** tab on the people directory fills in. The
applicant's page now reads "You're an approved foster", with a link to **My
Foster Animals**, instead of the application.

If the person already has a profile (paused, inactive, or added directly),
approving a new application sets it back to **Active** and replaces their
species and capabilities with the application's.

Nothing tells the applicant, apart from what they see when they next open
their page. Get in touch yourself when you approve.

## If something goes wrong

- **"A reason for the status change is required."** Fill in **Reason for
  Status Change**.
- **"Cannot change a rejected application to approved."** (with the two
  statuses) The change isn't allowed from where the application is now.
  Reload the page and look at **Status History**.
- **"Foster application not found."** The application was deleted. Go back
  to the list.
- **"Missing or invalid fields. Failed to update foster application."**
  Reload the page and try again.
- **"Database Error: Failed to update foster application."** Nothing was
  saved. Try again. If it keeps happening, tell an admin.
- **"Access Denied. You do not have permission to perform this action."**
  Your account can't change statuses. Ask a staff member who manages fosters.
