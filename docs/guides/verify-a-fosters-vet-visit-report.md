## When to use this

Use this when a foster has reported a visit to an outside vet. The report
says what happened and may carry the vet's paperwork. Nothing from it is on
the animal's medical record until you record it.

## Find the reports waiting

1. In the sidebar, open **Medical**, then **Due**.
2. At the bottom, **Foster reports** lists each report not yet reviewed, by
   foster: the animal, the visit day and what the foster wrote.
3. Click a report. The animal's **Documents** page opens at that report,
   under **Foster reports**, marked "Waiting for review" (see the
   [glossary](./medical-status-glossary.md#foster-reports)). The paperwork is
   linked under it, and also listed under **Documents**.

## Record the care from the report

1. Open the paperwork and read what the vet did.
2. On the report, click **Record care…**.
3. If the visit covered care already scheduled, click it under **Record care
   already scheduled**. Otherwise pick its kind under **Add an entry**:
   **Vaccine**, **Test**, **Treatment**, **Surgery**, **Exam** or
   **Microchip**.
4. The form opens with **Outside vet**, the vet's name and the visit day
   filled in. Fill in the rest as for any entry, for example a test's
   **Results**.
5. Click its **Record** button, for example **Record Test**.

You should see "Entry recorded." The report lists it under **Entries recorded
from this report**. Repeat for each thing the paperwork supports.

## Mark the report reviewed

1. When everything is recorded, click **Mark reviewed** on the report.

You should see "Report marked reviewed." It reads "Reviewed by" and your
name, and leaves the Due page. The foster sees "Reviewed on" and the day.

## Dismiss a report

Dismiss a report when there is nothing to record, for example a nail trim.

1. On the report, click **Dismiss**.
2. Type why under **Reason for dismissal**.
3. Click **Dismiss report**.

You should see "Report dismissed." It reads "Dismissed by" your name and the
reason, and leaves the Due page. The report and its paperwork stay on the
record, and the foster sees "Dismissed:" with your reason.

## If something goes wrong

- **The Dismiss report button is greyed out.** Type a reason first.
- **"Pick a clinic or give the vet's name."** Care given by an outside vet
  must name the vet.
- **An entry was recorded from the wrong report.** Void it from the animal's
  **History** and record it again.
- **No Record care…, Mark reviewed or Dismiss.** Reviewing foster reports
  needs a staff member who manages medical care. Ask them.
