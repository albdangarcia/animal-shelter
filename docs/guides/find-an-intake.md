## When to use this

Use the **Intakes** list to see every arrival at the shelter: first intakes
and animals coming back. Each arrival is one row, so an animal that came back
has a row for each time it came in.

## Steps

1. In the sidebar, open **Intakes**. The newest intakes are at the top.
2. To find one animal, type its name in **Filter by animal name...**. The
   list shows only its intakes.
3. To see one kind of arrival, click **Type** and tick **Stray**, **Owner
   Surrender**, **Transfer In** or another type. Tick more than one to see
   them together. Press Esc to close it.
4. To see the oldest first, change **Newest First** to **Oldest First**.
5. Once a name or a type is set, a **Reset** button appears. Click it to
   clear the name, the types and the order, and see the whole list again.

Each row shows:

- **Intake Date**: the day the animal arrived.
- **Animal**: its name. Click it to open the animal.
- **Type**: how it came in.
- **Source**: who or where it came from, with a label: a person
  ("Surrendered by"), a partner ("Partner") or a place ("Found"). "N/A" when
  the type has no source, for example **Born In Care**.
- **Recorded By**: the staff member who recorded the intake.

Use **Customize Columns** to hide columns, and **Rows per page** to show more
at once.

## If something goes wrong

- **No Intakes in the sidebar, or Access Denied.** Your account can't see
  intakes. Ask an admin.
- **The animal isn't on the list.** The filter matches the animal's name
  only. Check the spelling, and check **Type** in case a type is ticked.
- **A detail is wrong.** Staff who take animals in can correct it. Ask them.
