## When to use this

Use this after an animal has had surgery: a spay or neuter, a dental, or
another procedure. Use it too when an animal arrived already spayed or
neutered, or when it isn't to be spayed or neutered for now and you need to
say why.

The animal's spay/neuter status (**Altered**, **Exempt**, **Intact** or **Not
on record**) is worked out from these entries. See the
[glossary](./medical-status-glossary.md#spay-and-neuter).

## Record a scheduled spay or neuter

1. Open the animal, then the **Medical** tab.
2. Under **Due**, find the spay or neuter and click **Record**.
3. The form **Record Neuter as given** (or **Spay**) opens. **Procedure** is
   filled in.
4. Under **Given**, pick **In house** or **Outside vet**. For **Outside vet**,
   name the vet under **Vet clinic** or **Vet or clinic name**.
5. Check **Date given**.
6. Fill in what you know: **Surgeon**, **Findings**, **Anesthesia notes**,
   **Notes**.
7. Click **Record Surgery**.

You should see "Entry recorded." On **Overview**, **Spay/neuter** reads, for
example, "Altered (neuter, Nov 10, 2026)", and the row leaves **Due**.

## Record a surgery that wasn't scheduled

1. Open the animal, then the **Medical** tab.
2. Click **Add entry**, then **Surgery**.
3. Under **Procedure**, pick what was done. Only the spay or the neuter that
   fits the animal's sex is offered.
4. Under **Given**, pick **In house**, **Outside vet** or **Before intake**.
5. Check **Date given**, and fill in anything else you know.
6. Click **Record Surgery**.

You should see "Entry recorded." The entry appears under **Recent entries**.

## Record a spay or neuter done before intake

Use this when the animal arrived already spayed or neutered.

1. Click **Add entry**, then **Surgery**.
2. Under **Procedure**, pick **Spay** or **Neuter**.
3. Under **Given**, pick **Before intake**.
4. If nobody knows the day, tick **Date unknown**. Otherwise pick the **Date
   given**.
5. Under **Evidence**, pick what shows it was done: **Owner records**, **Vet
   records**, **Tattoo** or **Scar**.
6. Click **Record Surgery**.

You should see "Entry recorded." **Spay/neuter** reads, for example,
"Altered (spay, date unknown)".

An exam that finds the animal already altered can record this for you: see
[Record an exam](./record-an-exam.md).

## Record an exemption

Use this when the animal isn't to be spayed or neutered, for now or for good.

1. Click **Add entry**, then **Spay/neuter exemption…**.
2. **Exempt from** shows **Spay** or **Neuter**, from the animal's sex.
3. Under **Reason for the exemption**, pick **Too young or underweight**,
   **Medical risk**, **Partner will alter** or **Other**. For **Other**, say
   why under **Note**.
4. Pick a **Review date** if the decision should be looked at again, for
   example when a puppy is old enough. Left empty, the exemption holds until
   the spay or neuter is recorded, or the exemption is voided.
5. Click **Record exemption**.

You should see "Exemption recorded." **Spay/neuter** reads, for example,
"Exempt: medical risk", or with a review date, "Exempt until Jan 15, 2027:
too young or underweight". From 7 days before the review date, the Due page
shows a **Spay/neuter exemption review** row for it.

## Decline a scheduled spay or neuter

Declining a scheduled spay or neuter records an exemption.

1. Under **Due**, find the spay or neuter and click **Decline**.
2. Under **Reason for the exemption**, pick the reason. Add a **Note** and a
   **Review date** if you have them.
3. Click **Decline Entry**.

You should see "Entry declined." The entry stays on **History** as declined,
and **Spay/neuter** reads, for example, "Exempt: too young or underweight"
(or "Exempt until" the review date, if you picked one). To end the exemption
later, record the spay or neuter. Scheduling one doesn't end it.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Pick the procedure."** Pick what was done under **Procedure**.
- **"Say what shows the surgery was done before intake."** Pick the
  **Evidence**.
- **"The date given is required. Only care given before intake can have an
  unknown date."** Pick a date, or pick **Before intake** if nobody knows
  when it was done.
- **"Pick a clinic or give the vet's name."** Surgery by an outside vet must
  name the vet.
- **"Pick the reason for the exemption."** Pick a reason.
- **"Say why the animal is exempt."** You picked **Other**: fill in **Note**.
- **"This animal has left the shelter's care, so no exemption can be recorded
  for it."** The animal has an outcome.
- **No Decline on a spay or neuter, or no Add entry.** Declining a spay or
  neuter and recording care nobody scheduled need a staff member who manages
  medical care. Ask them.
- **The wrong surgery or exemption was recorded.** Staff who manage medical
  care can correct or void it from **History**.
