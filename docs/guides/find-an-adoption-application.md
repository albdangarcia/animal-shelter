## When to use this

Use this to find an application: everything waiting for a decision, one
person's applications, or everyone who applied for one animal. You can read
the lists with a read-only account. Deciding an application needs another
permission: see "Review an application and change its status" in
**Help**. What each status means is in
[the glossary](./adoption-application-status-glossary.md).

## Find an application on the Adoption Applications list

1. In the sidebar, click **Adoption Applications**. The newest applications
   are at the top. Each row shows the **Applicant Name**, **Applicant
   Email**, **Applicant Phone**, **Status**, **Animal Name**, **Animal
   Species** and when it was **Submitted At**.
2. Type part of a name in **Filter by applicant or animal name...**. The
   list shows only applications whose applicant or animal name contains it.
   Several animals share a name, so check the species and the applicant's
   email.
3. To see one status, click **Status** and tick one or more of **Pending**,
   **Reviewing**, **Waitlisted**, **Approved**, **Rejected**, **Withdrawn**,
   **Adopted** and **Closed**.
4. A **Reset** button appears once a name or status is set. Click it to see
   the whole list again.
5. To sort, click a column's name, then **Asc** or **Desc**. Use **Customize
   Columns** to hide columns, and **Rows per page** to show more.

Each row has a **…** button with **Review**, which opens the application. If
your account can only read applications, the rows have no **…** button.

## Find the applications for one animal

1. Open the animal from the **Animals** list.
2. Click its **Applications** tab.

The tab lists every application for that animal, with the same columns and
**Status** filter. **Filter by applicant name...** narrows the list. The
animal's card shows how many applications it has.

## Find the applications for one person

1. In the sidebar, open **People Directory** and click the person.
2. Click the **Adoption Applications** tab.

Anyone who can read people's records can open the tab. The **…** button and
**Add Application** show only to staff who can edit them.

It lists the animal, its species, the application's **Status**, when it was
**Submitted** and when it was **Last Updated** (any change counts, including
a note). Its **…** button has **Review** and, while the application can
still be edited, **Edit**. **Add Application** enters a new one for this
person.

## If something goes wrong

- **No results.** Nothing matches. Click **Reset**, or check the spelling of
  the name.
- **Access Denied** ("You do not have the necessary permissions to view this
  page."). Your account can't read applications, or the person's record. Ask
  a staff member who can.
- **The application you expect isn't there.** Check the animal's name and
  the person's email. If someone applied for a different animal with the same
  name, it is on that animal's tab.
