## When to use this

Use this when you've found an animal you would like to adopt. You fill in
one form about you and your home, and the shelter reviews it. Sending an
application doesn't mean the animal is yours yet: the shelter looks at it
first and gets in touch.

You can apply for as many animals as you like, but only once for each one.
After you apply, see
[Check, change, withdraw or reactivate your adoption application](./check-edit-withdraw-or-reactivate-your-application.md)
to follow it.

## Steps

1. Sign in. If you aren't signed in when you start, you are asked to sign in
   and then brought back to the form.
2. On the **My Adoption Applications** page, click **Adopt Pets**. You can
   also find the animals from the shelter's website.
3. Open the animal's page and click its **Adopt** button, for example
   **Adopt Duke**.
4. The form **Adoption Application** opens, in three sections. Fields marked
   with a star are required.
   - **Applicant Information**: your name, email, phone, address, city, state
     and ZIP code. The email is the address the shelter will use for this
     application. It does not change the email you sign in with.
   - **Home & Lifestyle**: **Living Situation**, **Household Size** (including
     you), whether you have a yard, whether you have landlord permission (if you
     rent), whether there are children (and their ages) and any **Other
     Animals** at home.
   - **Experience & Intent**: your **Animal Experience** and your **Reason for
     Adoption**.
5. Read your answers through. The form may start with details the shelter
   already has for you, such as your contact details and household answers, so
   check that they are still true. Your email starts as the one you sign in
   with. Write your **Reason for Adoption** fresh each time.
6. Click **Submit Application**.

You should see "Application submitted successfully." and **My Adoption
Applications** opens with your application at the top, reading **Pending**.
The shelter saves your name, phone and address to your profile as well, so you
don't have to type them next time.

## What the animal's page shows

- **Adopt** is replaced by **View Your Application** once you have an
  application for the animal, even a rejected or withdrawn one. It opens your
  **My Adoption Applications** list.
- **Pending Adoption** (greyed out) means the shelter is holding the animal
  for an adoption. That may be an application they accepted, including yours:
  check **My Adoption Applications** to see. You can't apply right now. If the
  adoption falls through and you have no application for the animal, the
  **Adopt** button comes back.

## If something goes wrong

- **A message under a field**, for example "Reason for adoption is required",
  "Invalid ZIP code" (five digits), "Please provide
  the ages of the children if 'Yes' is selected." or "Landlord permission is
  required when renting." Fix the field and click **Submit Application**
  again.
- **"You already have an application for this animal."** You have already
  applied. Open it from **My Adoption Applications**. If it was rejected, you
  can't apply for the same animal again, but you are welcome to apply for
  others.
- **"This animal is no longer available for adoption."** The shelter accepted
  another application, or the animal left, while you were filling in the form.
  Nothing was sent. Open the animal's page to check.
- **"Database Error: Failed to submit application. Please try again."**
  Nothing was sent. Try again. If it keeps happening, contact the shelter.
